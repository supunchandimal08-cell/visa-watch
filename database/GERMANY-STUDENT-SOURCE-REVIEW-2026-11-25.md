# Germany Student Immigration Source Review — 25 November 2026

**Status:** Research refresh completed; all dashboard rules remain **Pending human review**. This is a counsellor reference, not a legal decision or visa guarantee.

## 1. Student route and eligibility
- A third-country national generally applies for a national long-stay student visa before travel, then obtains/collects the appropriate residence permit from the competent local foreigners authority after arrival.
- Section 16b AufenthG covers full-time study at a state or state-recognised higher education institution or comparable institution. It also covers specified preparatory measures and compulsory internships.
- Admission (including eligible conditional admission/preparatory route), programme-specific language proficiency and adequate living funds must be checked.
- Residence permits are normally issued for two years; if the programme is shorter, for the programme duration. Extension may be possible if the study purpose has not yet been achieved and can reasonably be achieved within an appropriate period. Renewal is not automatic.

## 2. 2026 funds and blocked account
- German Embassy Colombo's student checklist currently states **EUR 11,904 per year / EUR 992 per month** for study commencement in 2026.
- Embassy-listed alternatives include a scholarship or a German sponsor's formal Declaration of Commitment (*Verpflichtungserklärung*). Verify whether the specific evidence is accepted in the applicant's case.
- A blocked account normally limits withdrawals to the monthly amount. Provider fees are additional; compare providers carefully. The Federal Foreign Office does not recommend or guarantee providers.
- Do not confuse the study amount (EUR 992/month) with the 2026 amount for a visa to seek a university place (EUR 1,091/month). Tuition and semester contributions are separate budget items where applicable.
- Recheck the amount for the actual application/start year before transferring funds. Do not count anticipated part-time earnings as initial proof of funds.

## 3. Work during studies
- Under Section 16b AufenthG, student employment is limited to **140 full working days or 280 half working days per calendar year**.
- A day with up to four hours can count as a half day; longer than four hours as a full day. A favourable weekly calculation can alternatively count lecture-period work of up to 20 hours/week and lecture-free periods under the statutory day-account method.
- Student assistant employment (studentische Nebentätigkeiten) is not counted toward the statutory day account.
- Compulsory internships are included in the study purpose; other internships and self-employment can require permission or separate assessment. Verify the exact residence title, employment type and current legal conditions.
- Never promise employment or that part-time income will cover tuition/living costs.

## 4. After graduation
- Eligible graduates who successfully complete a German higher-education programme may apply under Section 20(1) No. 1 AufenthG for a job-search residence permit of up to **18 months**.
- Evidence includes successful completion, health insurance and secured livelihood. During this permit, any gainful employment is allowed while seeking qualified work.
- The 18-month permit is not renewable beyond the statutory maximum. The graduate must apply to the competent foreigners authority; it is not an automatic extension.

## 5. Sri Lankan application route
- The German Embassy in Colombo is the relevant mission for applicants residing in Sri Lanka. Sri Lankan citizens need a visa for long-term study.
- Student applications are handled through the Federal Foreign Office **Consular Services Portal**. Online submission does not remove the in-person appointment: the Embassy says biometrics, original-document checks and fee payment still take place in person.
- The current student checklist requests, among other things: passport valid at least six months; national visa application and declaration; three biometric photographs; copies of used passport pages; motivation letter and CV; authenticated O/L and A/L certificates; admission/conditional admission; language evidence; proof of funds; and, where applicable, proof of study-fee payment. Master's applicants should include the Bachelor's degree documentation.
- The Embassy currently lists a national visa fee of **EUR 75**, payable in Sri Lankan rupees at the applicable exchange rate. The fee is non-refundable if refused.
- The Embassy's general national-visa page says processing is generally about **three months**; the student checklist says at least several weeks. Do not promise a decision date; advise applicants to plan well ahead and use the live student checklist/portal instructions.
- The Embassy asks applicants to prepare **two identical application sets** and bring original certificates. Incomplete applications may be delayed or refused. Complete documentation does not guarantee approval.

## 6. Residence registration after arrival
- After arrival, register the new address with the local registration authority (*Bürgeramt / Einwohnermeldeamt*) within the applicable local deadline (commonly within two weeks of moving in).
- Contact the local foreigners authority (*Ausländerbehörde*) promptly about the residence permit and follow the entry visa's validity conditions. Local appointment and documentation requirements vary.

## 7. Counsellor checklist
1. Confirm nationality, current residence, intended intake and competent German mission.
2. Verify the institution is state-recognised and the programme/admission route is eligible.
3. Confirm course language and accepted proof.
4. Calculate 2026 funds only if relevant to the 2026 start; confirm future-year figures before payment.
5. Separate blocked-account balance, provider fees, tuition, semester contribution, travel, insurance and initial accommodation costs.
6. Explain work limits accurately; do not market Germany as a guaranteed part-time-work or migration pathway.
7. Verify the current Embassy checklist, Consular Services Portal steps, appointment and document authentication.
8. Keep all legal interpretations Pending human review until an authorised reviewer signs off.
