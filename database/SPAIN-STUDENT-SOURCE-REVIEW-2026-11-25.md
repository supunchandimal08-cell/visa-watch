# Spain — Student Immigration Source Review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Status:** Research draft — all dashboard rules remain **Pending human review**.  
**Scope:** Non-EU/EEA/Swiss applicants, especially Sri Lankan residents.

## 1. Eligible study route
Stays over 90 days for study generally require long-term study-stay authorisation and a national study visa. Eligible categories include recognised higher education and specified post-compulsory secondary / higher vocational programmes. Provider recognition, course type, full-time status and required in-person/hybrid delivery must be checked against the current regulation. [Ministry of Inclusion — Study stay](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios) · [Royal Decree 1155/2024](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-24099)

## 2. Application timing and TIE
The general overseas route is through the Spanish diplomatic mission/consular office competent for the applicant's country of residence. The Ministry's current guidance says apply at least two months before course start, subject to limited exceptions. Certain applicants already legally in Spain may apply in-country for higher studies if the statutory conditions are met. For stays over six months, the TIE must be requested personally within one month after entry. Verify the exact New Delhi/BLS submission workflow for Sri Lanka. [Ministry guidance](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios)

## 3. Documents and insurance
Typical evidence includes passport, admission to a recognised full-time programme, registration-fee evidence, sufficient funds for stay and return, Spanish-compliant health insurance and application/fee forms. For stays over six months, adult criminal-record checks and applicable medical documentation may be required. Foreign public documents may need apostille/legalisation and sworn Spanish translation. Use the current Sri Lanka/New Delhi checklist for exact requirements.

## 4. Financial means — 2026 reference
A Spanish consular page consulted for 2026 states 100% IPREM = EUR600/month for the student, plus EUR450/month for the first accompanying family member and EUR300/month for each additional family member. This is a minimum immigration financial-means reference, not an all-in living-cost budget; tuition and return travel remain separate. The cited IPREM page is from a consular mission outside Sri Lanka, so confirm the same calculation and acceptable evidence with New Delhi before advising. [2026 consular guidance](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/washington/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/study-visa.aspx)

## 5. Work while studying
Current Ministry Sheet 4 bis states that study-stay work may not exceed 30 hours/week and must be compatible with studies. Higher-education study authorisation may automatically authorise employed or self-employed work within those conditions; other study categories may need a separate authorisation. Curriculum internships are treated separately. Do not promise a job or interpret 30 hours as guaranteed work. [Ministry Sheet 4 bis](https://ciudadaniaexterior.inclusion.gob.es/en/web/migraciones/w/hoja-4-bis-acceso-al-empleo-de-las-personas-titulares-de-una-autorizacion-de-estancia-de-larga-duracion-por-estudios-movilidad-de-alumnos-servicios-de-voluntariado-o-actividades-formativas)

## 6. Post-study
Eligible graduates who completed studies at an authorised Spanish higher-education institution and achieved at least EQF level 6 (degree level) may apply for a job-search/business-project residence authorisation for up to 24 months, non-extendable. It does not authorise work during its validity. A suitable job/business requires a subsequent appropriate authorisation. [Ministry Sheet 20](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/20.-autorizacion-de-residencia-para-busqueda-de-empleo-o-inicio-de-proyecto-empresarial)

## 7. Sri Lankan consular route and local checklist
Sri Lanka is within the Spanish Embassy in New Delhi's consular district. Colombo has an Honorary Consulate, not a career consular mission. The Embassy's current study-visa page directs applicants to submit at a BLS Visa Application Centre with a prior appointment; BLS's national-visa information lists Sri Lanka under New Delhi jurisdiction and includes the study visa over 90 days category. Confirm the currently designated Colombo centre, appointment route, fee, biometrics/personal appearance and document-return arrangements before every application. The New Delhi page's 2026 checklist states the student financial reference is EUR600/month (100% IPREM), EUR450/month for the first family member and EUR300/month for each additional family member; it also asks for the sponsor's original bank statements for the previous six months, signed and stamped by the bank. This is the more relevant consular checklist for Sri Lankan applicants than a different mission's local checklist. [Embassy consular districts](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Embajadas/nuevadelhi/en/Embajada/Paginas/Consulados.aspx) · [New Delhi study visa](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Embajadas/nuevadelhi/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/Visado-de-estudios.aspx) · [BLS national visas](https://india.blsspainvisa.com/national_visa.php/index.php)

## 8. Family accompaniment
The New Delhi Embassy's study-visa guidance lists the spouse or unmarried partner and children who are minors or who have a disability or serious disease as eligible accompanying family categories. Family-member applications are made after the student obtains the study visa. A family-member study visa does not itself authorise employment. Check relationship evidence, eligibility, funds and the current individual checklist. [New Delhi study visa](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Embajadas/nuevadelhi/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/Visado-de-estudios.aspx)

## 9. Student work — geographical condition
The Ministry's Sheet 4 bis confirms a maximum of 30 hours per week, compatible with studies. It also says the work authorisation is geographically limited to the autonomous community where the study centre is located, with an exception for localities in a neighbouring autonomous community that border it. Higher-education study authorisation may automatically permit employed or self-employed work; other study categories may need a separate authorisation. Curriculum internships are treated separately. [Ministry Sheet 4 bis — English](https://ciudadaniaexterior.inclusion.gob.es/en/web/migraciones/w/hoja-4-bis-acceso-al-empleo-de-las-personas-titulares-de-una-autorizacion-de-estancia-de-larga-duracion-por-estudios-movilidad-de-alumnos-servicios-de-voluntariado-o-actividades-formativas)

## 10. Decision period and practical timing
The New Delhi Embassy page states a legal decision period of one month from the day after submission; this can be extended where an interview or further documents are requested. This is not a guaranteed completion date. Apply at least two months before the programme starts and allow additional time for BLS appointment availability, document legalisation/translation and case-specific checks. [New Delhi study visa](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Embajadas/nuevadelhi/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/Visado-de-estudios.aspx)

## 11. Targeted official-source recheck — 26 November 2026

### Governing route and eligible studies
- The New Delhi Embassy's current English study-visa page distinguishes study stays exceeding 90 days from study stays of 90 days or less. The long-stay study visa is linked to the underlying study-stay authorisation; it is not a Schengen C visa.
- Royal Decree 1155/2024, Articles 34–35, covers the long-stay route and requires the relevant initial authorisation, valid passport, visa fee and sufficient maintenance/return resources. The regulation requires the passport to have at least one year validity for the visa application. The New Delhi checklist separately states two blank pages and rejects passports issued more than 10 years ago; use the competent mission's current checklist.
- The Embassy's page states applications should normally be lodged at least two months before the programme starts. It gives a one-month legal decision period from the day after submission, which can be extended for interview or further documents. This is not a guaranteed processing time.
- The Ministry guidance allows eligible higher-education applicants who are adults and legally in Spain to apply in-country subject to conditions. Do not present this as a general route for applicants abroad or for every study category.

### 2026 financial means and sponsorship
- The current Spanish Embassy New Delhi study-visa checklist explicitly states 2026 maintenance means of EUR 600/month for the student, EUR 450/month for the first accompanying family member and EUR 300/month for each additional family member.
- For a 12-month stay, the simple student-only benchmark is EUR 7,200 (600 × 12), before tuition, travel/return costs and other setup expenses. This is a calculated planning illustration, not a separate statutory lump-sum figure.
- The New Delhi checklist asks for original sponsor bank statements for the previous six months, signed and stamped by the bank, along with sponsor documentation. Where the full accommodation period is paid in advance, the maintenance amount may be reduced under the applicable rule/checklist. Tuition costs are not counted as maintenance funds.
- Keep the EUR 600 figure tied to the New Delhi Embassy's published 2026 checklist, not merely an unrelated consulate's page. Reconfirm it on the filing date.

### Work rights and geographical restriction
- Article 57 of Royal Decree 1155/2024 states that higher-education study-stay authorisation allows employed or self-employed work automatically, without an additional procedure, provided the work is compatible with the studies.
- General work activity is capped at 30 hours/week, except the specified intensive vocational-training regime. The same article limits the work authorisation geographically to the autonomous community where the study-stay authorisation was granted, with the statutory neighbouring-locality exception described in Ministry Sheet 4 bis.
- This is permission to work subject to conditions, not a job offer, placement service, income guarantee or basis to reduce required funds. Do not apply the higher-education automatic permission to every language, vocational or secondary course.

### Sri Lankan consular jurisdiction and operational caution
- The Spanish Embassy in New Delhi's official consular-district page explicitly includes Sri Lanka. Colombo is listed as an honorary consulate under New Delhi's jurisdiction; it is not the career consular office deciding national study visas.
- The New Delhi Embassy study-visa page directs applicants to BLS Visa Application Centres by prior appointment. However, the exact currently designated submission centre and practical acceptance arrangements for a Sri Lankan resident must be confirmed directly before booking or travel. Do not assume an India-based VAC is accessible to a Sri Lankan applicant solely because the Embassy page refers to BLS.
- The Embassy checklist includes a Spanish-authorised health insurer with broad cover equivalent to Spain's public healthcare, without deductible, waiting period or coverage limit. For stays exceeding 180 days, adult applicants generally need criminal-record certificates covering countries of residence in the previous five years and a medical certificate. Legalisation/apostille and official Spanish translation may apply.
- Do not copy the Embassy's India-specific instruction for Indian Police Clearance Certificates and apostille into a Sri Lankan applicant checklist. Obtain the current Sri Lanka-specific police certificate, medical certificate, legalisation chain and translation instructions from the Embassy.

### Post-study pathway
- Article 190(10) refers eligible graduates of Spanish higher-education institutions who reach at least EQF level 6 to the job-search / business-project residence authorisation under Additional Provision 17 of Law 14/2013. The separate Ministry Sheet 20 sets the route conditions and duration; it does not authorise work while held. A subsequent suitable work/business authorisation is required.
- This pathway is not available automatically to every course completer and must not be described as a guaranteed job or residence outcome.
