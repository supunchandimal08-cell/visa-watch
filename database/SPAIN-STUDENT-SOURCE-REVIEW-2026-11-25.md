# Spain — Student Immigration Source Review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Status:** Research draft — all dashboard rules remain **Pending human review**.  
**Scope:** Non-EU/EEA/Swiss applicants, especially Sri Lankan residents.

## 1. Eligible study route
Stays over 90 days for study generally require long-term study-stay authorisation and a national study visa. Eligible categories include recognised higher education and specified post-compulsory secondary / higher vocational programmes. Provider recognition, course type, full-time status and required in-person/hybrid delivery must be checked against the current regulation. [Ministry of Inclusion — Study stay](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios) · [Royal Decree 1155/2024](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2024-24099)

## 2. Application timing and TIE
The general overseas route is through the Spanish diplomatic mission/consular office competent for the applicant's country of residence. The Ministry's current guidance says apply at least two months before course start, subject to limited exceptions. Certain applicants already legally in Spain may apply in-country for higher studies if the statutory conditions are met. For stays over six months, the TIE must be requested personally within one month after entry. Verify the exact New Delhi/BLS submission workflow for Sri Lanka. [Ministry guidance](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios)

## 3. Documents and insurance
Typical evidence includes passport, admission to a recognised full-time programme, registration-fee evidence, sufficient funds for stay and return, Spanish-compliant health insurance and application/fee forms. For stays over six months, adult criminal-record checks and applicable medical documentation may be required. Foreign public documents may need apostille/legalisation and sworn Spanish translation. Use the current Sri Lanka/New Delhi checklist for exact requirements.

## 4. Financial means — 2026 reference
A Spanish consular page consulted for 2026 states 100% IPREM = EUR600/month for the student, plus EUR450/month for the first accompanying family member and EUR300/month for each additional family member. This is a minimum immigration financial-means reference, not an all-in living-cost budget; tuition and return travel remain separate. The cited IPREM page is from a consular mission outside Sri Lanka, so confirm the same calculation and acceptable evidence with New Delhi before advising. [2026 consular guidance](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/washington/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/study-visa.aspx)

## 5. Work while studying
Current Ministry Sheet 4 bis states that study-stay work may not exceed 30 hours/week and must be compatible with studies. Higher-education study authorisation may automatically authorise employed or self-employed work within those conditions; other study categories may need a separate authorisation. Curriculum internships are treated separately. Do not promise a job or interpret 30 hours as guaranteed work. [Ministry Sheet 4 bis](https://ciudadaniaexterior.inclusion.gob.es/en/web/migraciones/w/hoja-4-bis-acceso-al-empleo-de-las-personas-titulares-de-una-autorizacion-de-estancia-de-larga-duracion-por-estudios-movilidad-de-alumnos-servicios-de-voluntariado-o-actividades-formativas)

## 6. Post-study
Eligible graduates who completed studies at an authorised Spanish higher-education institution and achieved at least EQF level 6 (degree level) may apply for a job-search/business-project residence authorisation for up to 24 months, non-extendable. It does not authorise work during its validity. A suitable job/business requires a subsequent appropriate authorisation. [Ministry Sheet 20](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/migraciones/w/20.-autorizacion-de-residencia-para-busqueda-de-empleo-o-inicio-de-proyecto-empresarial)

## 7. Sri Lankan consular route
Sri Lanka is within the Spanish Embassy in New Delhi's consular district. Colombo has an Honorary Consulate. BLS's national-visa page lists Sri Lanka within New Delhi jurisdiction and shows a general student visa over 90 days category. Confirm current BLS Colombo submission arrangements, appointment, fees, personal appearance and residence conditions directly before advising. [Embassy consular districts](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Embajadas/nuevadelhi/es/Embajada/Paginas/Consulados.aspx) · [BLS national visas](https://india.blsspainvisa.com/national_visa.php/index.php)
