# UK Student Route — Financial Requirements

**Prepared:** 25 November 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom  
**Rule area:** Appendix Student ST 12; Appendix Finance; HC 584 APP ST8–ST10  
**Record status:** Needs human review before client advice  
**Primary authority:** Home Office / UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI)

## 1. What the applicant may need to show

For a typical Student entry-clearance application, assess separately:

1. Any outstanding course fees stated on the Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS).
2. Required living-cost funds for the study location.
3. Any additional required funds for eligible dependants, if applicable.
4. Whether an exemption or alternative evidence route applies.

Do not assume that tuition paid to an agent, a conditional offer, or a general bank balance automatically satisfies the Rules. Confirm the amount actually recorded by the sponsor on the CAS and the evidence accepted under the current Rules.

## 2. Student living-cost figures and date control

The current Appendix Student text displayed on 25 November 2026 shows:

| Study location | Monthly amount currently shown | Maximum calculation |
|---|---:|---:|
| London | £1,529 | Up to 9 months |
| Outside London | £1,171 | Up to 9 months |

HC 584, APP ST8 and APP ST9, substitutes the figures with effect from **30 November 2026**:

| Study location | From 30 November 2026 | Maximum calculation |
|---|---:|---:|
| London | £1,570 per month | Up to 9 months |
| Outside London | £1,203 per month | Up to 9 months |

HC 584 says applications made before 30 November 2026 are decided under the Rules in force on 29 November 2026 in relation to these changes. Confirm the actual application date and recheck for later amendments before calculating an individual case.

**Important:** GOV.UK's separate financial-evidence guidance page still displayed the earlier £1,529 / £1,171 figures and showed last updated 31 December 2025 when checked on 25 November 2026. Do not rely on that page alone for an application on or after 30 November. Cross-check the amended Rules and current official guidance.

## 3. Calculation method — typical case

For a typical applicant who is not exempt:

- Outstanding course fees: amount due for the relevant academic year as shown on the CAS (subject to the Rules).
- Living costs: applicable monthly amount × number of months required, up to 9 months.
- Round a part-month up to the next full month where the Rules require it.
- Check any permitted accommodation-deposit offset and the applicable cap in the Rules.
- Add dependant funds separately if dependants are eligible and applying.

Do not use a single generic total for all applicants. Study location, course duration, fees already paid, sponsor-provided accommodation, dependants and exemptions can change the calculation.

## 4. Holding period and evidence

For the standard personal-funds route, the required funds generally must be held for **28 consecutive days** in accordance with Appendix Finance. The evidence date must meet the current timing rule; the GOV.UK evidence guidance states the evidence must be dated no more than 31 days before the application.

Check the current Rules and guidance for:
- Account holder and control of funds.
- Immediate access to funds.
- Permitted financial institutions and evidence formats.
- Parent/partner funds and relationship / consent evidence.
- Official financial sponsorship and student-loan routes.
- Currency conversion method and application-date exchange rate.
- Any nationality-based differential evidence exemption.

Do not treat a screenshot or an informal balance message as sufficient proof without checking the accepted evidence rules.

## 5. Common exemptions / alternative routes to assess

Appendix Student includes circumstances in which the applicant may not need to show the standard funds, including certain applicants who have held permission in the UK for at least 12 months, Student Union Sabbatical Officers and other specifically defined cases. Nationality-based differential evidence arrangements may also affect whether documents must be submitted, but do not assume this removes the underlying financial requirement.

Check the exact current provision and applicant facts. Never apply an exemption based only on nationality, a previous visa label, or an agent's statement.
