# Romania — Student Immigration Source Review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Status:** Research draft — all dashboard rules remain **Pending human review**.  
**Scope:** Non-EU/EEA applicants, including Sri Lankan nationals; full-time higher education and doctoral study.

## 1. Main student route
A non-EU applicant must first obtain admission to a Romanian state or private higher-education institution that is accredited or provisionally authorised. For higher education, the Ministry of Education's Letter of Acceptance for full-time study is a key requirement. The applicant then applies for a national long-stay study visa, category D/SD, through Romania's e-VISA portal and the Romanian diplomatic mission/consular post with territorial competence. The D/SD visa is issued for 90 days. After arrival, the student applies to the General Inspectorate for Immigration (IGI) for a temporary residence permit for studies. A short-stay C visa is not the route for a full academic programme.

## 2. Sri Lanka application channel
The EU consular representation directory lists an Embassy of Romania in Colombo, 25/1 Horton Place, Colombo 7, with official contact email colombo@mae.ro and telephone contacts. Romania's e-VISA guidance says applications must be lodged at the Romanian diplomatic mission or consular post competent for the applicant's domicile/residence; online validation is followed by an appointment/interview and in-person presentation of original documents. Therefore, do not assume New Delhi or an outsourced VFS centre for Sri Lankan residents. Confirm directly with the Embassy of Romania in Colombo and the live e-VISA portal which mission will accept the individual application, current appointment procedure, fees and document checklist.

## 3. D/SD visa documents and funds
The IGI and Romanian MFA e-VISA guidance lists, for higher-education students: Ministry of Education Letter of Acceptance for full-time study at an accredited/provisionally authorised institution; proof of tuition payment for at least one academic year; means of support at least equal to the national gross minimum basic salary per month for the entire period entered in the visa; criminal-record certificate; medical insurance covering the visa period; valid passport and photographs. The current IGI page lists the long-stay visa fee as EUR 300. Romanian state scholarship beneficiaries have exemptions from certain tuition/funds/consular-fee requirements. Do not convert the salary threshold to a fixed RON amount without checking the minimum wage in force on the application date. This visa-stage financial rule is distinct from the residence-permit financial evidence.

## 4. Residence permit after arrival
After entering Romania, the student must apply personally to the territorial IGI unit for the county of residence, or use the official IGI online portal where available. The IGI page instructs students to apply at least 30 days before the visa/right of stay expires. Documents include passport, acceptance letter for the first extension, university certificate confirming enrolment in a full-time programme, means of support of at least the national gross minimum salary for at least six months, proof of legal accommodation, medical certificate and applicable fees. The permit can cover the study period and is renewable while the conditions remain met. Confirm the current residence-card fee and local appointment process with IGI.

## 5. Work during study
The current IGI “Studies” page states that foreign students holding temporary residence for study may work without a separate work permit under an individual part-time employment contract for no more than 6 hours per day. Do not present this as unrestricted full-time work, self-employment or a guarantee of a job. The IGI's older/alternate English guidance has shown a 4-hour wording, while the current dedicated “Studies” page states 6 hours; this source discrepancy should be recorded and reconfirmed with IGI before giving client-specific advice. The Study in Romania government portal also states 6 hours/day.

## 6. After graduation
IGI states that the study residence right may be extended for up to 6 months to complete the academic situation and authenticate study documents. For graduates who have completed studies, the residence right may be extended for 9 months to seek employment, complete legal employment formalities or open a business, on presentation of documents proving completion. The 9-month route is not a job offer, work permit or guarantee of employment; the graduate must meet the applicable conditions and complete the correct change/extension process before the current status expires.

## 7. Schengen distinction
Romania became a full Schengen member on 1 January 2025. However, a Romanian national long-stay D/SD visa is a Romanian national visa for entry/stay in Romania; it does not itself confer a right to reside or study long-term in another Schengen state. Students must obtain the relevant residence status for their actual destination and respect Schengen short-stay rules when travelling elsewhere.

## 8. Long-term residence — do not promise settlement
IGI's 7 July 2026 information states that study-residence time is counted at half when calculating long-term residence. The applicant must also meet the other statutory conditions, including a qualifying non-study residence status, continuous residence, absence limits, insurance, means, accommodation and Romanian-language knowledge. Do not tell students that years on a study permit automatically count as full years toward permanent residence.

## Counsellor checklist
- Verify the university's accreditation/provisional authorisation, exact campus, programme level, full-time status, duration and Ministry Letter of Acceptance.
- Confirm tuition payment/refund rules, academic entry requirements, language requirements and genuine study rationale directly with the institution.
- Use the e-VISA portal and confirm territorial competence and appointment instructions with the Romanian Embassy in Colombo; do not assume VFS or New Delhi.
- Calculate funds using the current gross minimum wage and the exact visa/residence period; keep visa-stage and residence-stage requirements distinct.
- Check criminal record, insurance, passport validity, accommodation, medical certificate, translations and legalisation requirements.
- Explain the work limit as part-time only; reconfirm the IGI 4h/6h discrepancy before client advice.
- Explain the 9-month post-graduation option is a temporary job-search/business-formalities extension, not employment or permanent residence.
- Record source URL, access date, publication/effective date, reviewer and decision. Keep all rules Pending human review until approved.
