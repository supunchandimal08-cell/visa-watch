# Netherlands Student Residence Permit — Official Source Review (25 November 2026)

## Scope and review status
This review is for non-EU/EEA applicants considering higher education in the Netherlands from Sri Lanka. Nationality, programme, sponsor, exact residence endorsement and application date matter. This is a counsellor reference, not legal advice. Dashboard rules remain **Pending human review**.

## 1. Eligibility and recognised sponsor
For a university or university of applied sciences (HBO) route, the student must generally:
- Have proof of registration or temporary registration.
- Be admitted to an accredited full-time programme.
- Study at an institution recognised by the IND as a sponsor.
- Meet the study-income requirement.
- Make sufficient progress: generally at least 50% of the credits for each study year.

The educational institution, not the student or overseas agent, submits the student residence application and, where required, the MVV application. Check the institution in the IND public register (updated monthly); verify the exact legal entity and programme, not just a marketing brand.

The IND page states a €254 application fee and a 60-day decision period for the student residence route. The decision period may be extended, for example if the application is incomplete. Confirm current fees and route-specific requirements before quoting.

## 2. Living funds — 2026
IND's study norm for 1 January–31 December 2026:
- University / higher professional education (HBO): **€1,130.77 per month**.
- Secondary / senior secondary vocational education (MBO): **€928.58 per month**.

The amount excludes tuition. The student must generally prove sufficient funds for 12 months. Accepted proof routes include money in the student's own freely accessible account, a deposit to the institution, scholarship, company funding or a private financier abroad using the IND's required declaration and supporting evidence. Check document age, account access, translation language, sponsor identity and exact institution process. Do not assume a family sponsorship letter alone is sufficient.

## 3. Work while studying
For a non-EEA student with a study residence endorsement, paid employment generally requires the employer to obtain a TWV (work permit). The student must choose either:
- Up to 16 hours per week during the year; or
- Full-time work in June, July and August.

A study-related internship can be exempt from a TWV when it is relevant to the Dutch study programme and the student, institution and employer have signed the required internship agreement. Work outside these conditions can breach permit/employment rules. EEA/Swiss nationals have different free-movement rights. Self-employment is treated differently and requires the student to continue meeting residence conditions and comply with registration/tax obligations. Never promise a job or earnings.

## 4. Study progress and permit validity
The institution monitors study progress. A student generally needs at least 50% of annual credits. If progress is insufficient, the institution considers whether there are valid reasons; if not, it must report the situation to the IND. The IND then assesses continued eligibility. The permit is generally valid for the duration of studies plus three months, up to a maximum of five years, and only while requirements continue to be met.

## 5. After graduation — orientation year
An eligible graduate/researcher may apply for a one-year orientation-year residence permit. It is not automatic and is not available to every foreign graduate. Eligibility generally includes completing an accredited Dutch bachelor's/master's degree, qualifying post-master's programme, doctorate/research, Erasmus Mundus Joint Master, or certain degrees from a designated foreign institution. A foreign institution generally must rank in the top 200 in at least two of the specified global ranking publishers on the graduation date; foreign diploma evaluation by Nuffic and English-language criteria may apply. Apply within three years of qualifying graduation/research. The orientation-year permit is valid for one year, cannot be extended, and permits work without a TWV. Check the exact current IND conditions before presenting this as a pathway.

## 6. Sri Lanka application and counselling steps
1. Ask the institution to confirm it is an IND-recognised sponsor and that the exact programme is accredited and full-time.
2. Confirm the institution itself will submit the residence permit/MVV application and identify who receives funds and documents.
3. Confirm the student's nationality and whether an MVV is required; follow the institution's country-specific instructions and NetherlandsWorldwide for the Dutch representation appointment.
4. Prepare passport, admission/registration evidence, academic and language evidence, financial proof, translations/legalisation, and any TB test or other requirement identified by the institution/IND.
5. Explain the 2026 living-funds amount separately from tuition, insurance, travel and setup costs.
6. Explain work limits and employer TWV before the student makes a financial plan based on part-time work.
7. Explain that orientation year eligibility is conditional and requires a separate application.
8. Record source, date checked, reviewer and decision. Keep rules Pending human review until authorised approval.

## 7. Sri Lanka MVV and arrival procedure — 26 November 2026
- For the higher-education student route, the recognised educational institution submits the residence permit application and, where required, the MVV application to the IND. The student should not assume they can self-file a separate student MVV application outside the sponsor process.
- If the positive IND decision letter says the MVV sticker can be collected in Sri Lanka, the student books an appointment at the Dutch Embassy in Colombo. NetherlandsWorldwide states the appointment must be made within **3 months** after the IND positive decision.
- Bring passport, passport photograph, copy of the IND approval/decision letter or the institution's email containing the V-number, and all documents specified in the IND letter. Passport must be valid for at least **6 months** from appointment and have at least **2 blank pages**. The Embassy may take up to **10 working days** to return the passport with the MVV sticker.
- The MVV sticker is the entry document; the student residence card is collected separately after arrival when the IND notifies the institution/student.
- Official Sri Lanka process: https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/mvv-long-stay/apply-sri-lanka

## 8. Health insurance — study-only versus employment
- Study in NL guidance says a student who is in the Netherlands for study purposes only generally cannot take out Dutch public basic health insurance; the student needs suitable private or home-country medical cover valid in the Netherlands.
- Once the student takes a paid job, Dutch basic health insurance is required; international student insurance is not sufficient while employed. For self-employment, the student should ask the Sociale Verzekeringsbank (SVB) for an assessment of their Wlz insurance position.
- Paid internships can have a separate insurance result depending on remuneration and social-insurance status. Confirm the student's exact position with SVB before work starts.
- Official guidance: https://www.studyinnl.org/plan-your-stay/healthcare-insurance ; Government.nl https://www.government.nl/themes/migration-and-travel/immigration-to-the-netherlands/question-and-answer/health-insurance/when-do-i-need-to-take-out-health-insurance-if-i-come-to-live-in-the-netherlands

## 9. Family members
- The IND student residence page says the recognised educational institution can also submit residence applications for a student's family members. The family members need their own residence permissions and must meet the applicable requirements; the student remains responsible for their lawful stay.
- The IND page states that family members under the student route are **not allowed to work**. Do not promise automatic approval or work rights. Confirm each dependant's requirements and financial evidence with the institution/IND.

## 10. Reconfirmed 2026 financial and study conditions
- The IND 2026 study norm for university/HBO is **EUR 1,130.77/month**, or **EUR 13,569.24 for 12 months**. The amount excludes tuition. The MBO amount is EUR 928.58/month; do not use it for university/HBO applicants.
- The study norm is updated annually. The student must prove the amount for 12 months and repeat the income evidence each study year as required by the institution.
- For university/HBO, the study permit requires full-time accredited programme, IND-recognised sponsor and at least **50% of annual credits** (subject to the institution's assessment of valid personal circumstances).
- The IND lists the student application fee at **EUR 254** and a **60-day** decision period, which can be extended if the application is incomplete.
- Student work: up to **16 hours/week** during the year or full-time in June, July and August, not both; employer must obtain a TWV. A qualifying study-related internship can be exempt when the required agreement is signed by student, institution and employer.
- The orientation-year permit is valid for one year, cannot be extended, and allows work without a TWV. Eligibility must be checked under the IND's detailed criteria and application window (generally within 3 years of qualifying graduation/research).
