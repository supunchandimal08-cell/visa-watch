## Rule notes for counsellors

### 1. Genuine Student criterion

- Applies to Student visa applications lodged on or after 23 March 2024; earlier applications were assessed under the former Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) requirement.
- Current online form asks four main questions, in English, with a 150-word limit per response. They cover current circumstances/ties, why the course and provider in Australia, the course's benefit, and other relevant information. Additional question(s) may apply based on visa history and application location.
- Attach relevant supporting evidence. The Department says it gives more weight to claims supported by evidence.
- Review academic history, study gaps, provider/course research, employment, family/community/economic circumstances and immigration history. Avoid copied generic statements and invented career plans.

### 2. Financial capacity

The Home Affairs page currently publishes these 12-month living-cost figures:
- Student: AUD 29,710
- Partner: AUD 10,394
- Dependent child: AUD 4,449
- School-age dependent's schooling costs are additional (published guide amount AUD 13,502 per year).

Also consider first 12 months of tuition (or pro-rata course fees), travel, and applicable schooling costs. Deduct tuition already paid only when supported by evidence. The official page explains that genuine access to funds and the source/accumulation of deposits may need to be shown; a one-day bank balance certificate alone does not demonstrate how funds accumulated. An annual-income alternative has separate thresholds and acceptable government tax evidence. The Document Checklist Tool is applicant-specific. Do not treat these minimum figures as a complete cost-of-living budget or promise that meeting them guarantees a visa.

### 3. Work rights

- General Student visa guidance: up to 48 hours per fortnight while the course is in session.
- Students generally cannot work before their course starts.
- Unlimited hours may apply during scheduled course breaks and, after course commencement, to students undertaking a master's by research or doctoral degree.
- Check the actual visa grant notice and VEVO condition(s), including condition 8105. Never promise a job, income or that work can finance visa eligibility.

### 4. CoE, CRICOS and OSHC

- Confirm a valid Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) for each intended course; the CoE must remain valid when the Department decides the application.
- Check the exact provider, course, campus/location, CRICOS code and registration dates. Provider registration alone does not prove every course/campus is eligible.
- Adequate Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is generally required for the visa period for the applicant and included family members unless an official exemption applies. Check provider-arranged policy dates and avoid gaps.
- English evidence/exemptions are separate from provider admission standards; use the applicant-specific checklist and current instrument.

### 5. Application location and family-member reforms — 2 October 2026

The 2026 amendment regulations commenced on 2 October 2026. The reforms narrow who may lodge or be granted a Student visa while in Australia and restrict when family members may be included. The exact rule depends on visa history, applicant class, location, family category and transitional provisions. Do not summarise this as an unconditional “everyone must apply offshore”; check the current Home Affairs rule page and instrument for the individual applicant. Applications made before commencement are subject to the relevant transitional provisions described in the instrument.

## 6. Current work-rights and Temporary Graduate cross-check — 25 November 2026

- The current Home Affairs / Study Australia guidance confirms the general cap is **48 hours per fortnight** while the course is in session. Students generally cannot work before their course starts. Unlimited work may apply during scheduled course breaks and, after course commencement, to master's by research and doctoral students. Family work rights differ; confirm condition 8105 and VEVO for each person.
- The Post-Higher Education Work stream of subclass 485 is a separate application, not an automatic student-visa extension. Home Affairs currently states applicants generally must be 35 or under, be in Australia at application, have held a Student visa in the previous six months, and have completed an eligible CRICOS-registered degree within six months. Exceptions apply to specified research Masters/doctoral graduates and Hong Kong/BNO passport holders.
- Current indicative stay periods: Bachelor 2 years; Masters coursework/extended 2 years; Masters research 3 years; Doctoral 3 years. The page currently lists the charge from AUD5,750. Applicants must also meet the Australian study requirement, English, health insurance, AFP check and all other stream requirements.
- The subclass 500 page currently lists the main applicant visa charge from **AUD2,500**, with extra charges for family members and possible additional medical, police and biometrics costs. Always use the live Visa Pricing Estimator; fees change.
- Study Australia says processing-time tools are indicative only and are based on recently decided applications, not a promise for the applicant.
