# Austria Student Immigration Source Review — 25 November 2026

**Status:** Country-specific review refreshed. All dashboard rules are **Pending human review**. This is a counsellor reference, not legal advice or a visa guarantee.

## 1. Correct route for a Sri Lankan student
- For a course longer than six months, the usual route for a third-country national is the **Residence Permit – Student (Aufenthaltsbewilligung – Student)**, not a long-stay Schengen student visa.
- The Austrian Embassy New Delhi has consular jurisdiction for Sri Lanka. Residence permit applications for stays exceeding six months must be lodged **in person at the Embassy in New Delhi**; not through VFS or an honorary consulate.
- The initial application is generally submitted before travel and the applicant waits abroad for the Austrian residence authority's decision. After approval, the Embassy informs the applicant and the applicant applies for a **Visa D** to enter Austria and collect the residence card. Visa D is an entry visa; it is not the residence permit itself.
- Sri Lankan citizens require a visa for entry. Do not advise tourist/Schengen visa entry as a shortcut to apply in Austria.
- For study up to six months, Visa C (up to 90 days) or Visa D (91–180 days) may apply depending on duration and purpose. These visas are not renewable in Austria.

## 2. Eligible studies
The Student residence permit can cover regular studies at Austrian universities, universities of applied sciences, accredited private universities and recognised teacher-training institutions; certain non-degree university/FH courses of at least 40 ECTS (not language-only); university preparation courses tied to supplementary admission examinations; certain nostrification/equivalence studies; and legally required professional training following a regular degree. Confirm institution, programme type, admission decision and any entrance examination before accepting a case.

## 3. 2026 financial means
OeAD's 2026 monthly minimums:
- Under 24: **EUR 722.58/month**.
- Age 24 or older: **EUR 1,308.39/month**.
- Married couple jointly: EUR 2,064.12/month; each child adds EUR 201.88/month.
- Funds generally have to be evidenced for the requested permit period, up to 12 months in advance, in an account/savings book in the applicant's name and accessible from Austria. A qualifying EU-resident guarantor, scholarship or other accepted evidence may be possible.
- The accommodation allowance included in the calculation is EUR 386.43/month. If accommodation costs are higher, the excess must be added to required funds. Health insurance costs and regular financial obligations also need to be considered.
- The authority may ask for proof of the origin of funds. OeAD examples include six months of bank statements, proof of applicant/sponsor income, scholarship confirmation, loan agreement or sale/inheritance evidence. Parental support may be combined with applicant savings if documented and freely available.
- These thresholds are not the full cost of studying or living in Austria. Tuition/semester contributions, rent, deposits, food, travel, insurance and setup costs are additional. Recheck figures for the actual application year.

## 4. Health insurance and accommodation
- The student needs health insurance covering all risks in Austria. Travel insurance is normally needed for entry/transition, but is not a substitute for full residence-period insurance.
- Eligible enrolled degree students and certain university-preparation students may take ÖGK student self-insurance after arrival. OeAD lists the 2026 premium as **EUR 78.84/month**; eligibility conditions apply.
- OeAD's checklist requires accommodation evidence for at least three months. Accommodation cost also affects the funds calculation.
- The authority may request additional evidence in an individual case.

## 5. Work while studying
- Third-country students need an employment permit (*Beschäftigungsbewilligung*), including for marginal/low-hours employment.
- The employer applies to the Austrian Public Employment Service (AMS) before the student starts work.
- Up to **20 hours per week** may be approved without a labour-market test. More than 20 hours may require an AMS labour-market test and must not jeopardise the primary purpose of study.
- Never tell clients that the student can start work immediately on arrival, or that part-time work is guaranteed or will cover the whole cost of study.

## 6. Renewal and academic progress
- The Student residence permit is normally valid for **12 months**; eligible EU/multilateral mobility programme cases may receive two years.
- Apply for renewal before expiry, and no earlier than three months before expiry.
- OeAD generally requires proof of study success of **8 semester hours or 16 ECTS per academic year**, plus continuing enrolment and other general requirements. Programme-specific exceptions (for example, doctoral study) must be checked.
- Renewal is not automatic.

## 7. After graduation
- Eligible third-country graduates who successfully complete a qualifying Austrian public university, university of applied sciences or accredited private university programme may renew their Student residence permit for **12 months** to seek employment or start a business.
- Eligible qualifications include Bachelor's, Master's, doctorate/PhD, or qualifying diploma programme from at least its second stage.
- General requirements such as adequate funds and all-risk health insurance continue to apply.
- A graduate with a qualifying job offer matching their qualification and meeting locally customary salary conditions may apply for a **Red-White-Red Card** without a labour-market test, subject to remaining legal requirements.
- This is not an automatic permanent residence permit or unrestricted work right.

## 8. Application timing, documents and arrival
- OeAD advises applying as early as possible and no later than **three months before planned arrival**; processing can take up to 90 days and may take longer if additional documents are requested.
- The Embassy recommends submitting a residence permit application at least three months before intended entry. Confirm appointment instructions directly with the Embassy at **NEW-DELHI-KA@bmeia.gv.at**.
- Common documents: signed application form, valid passport, recent ICAO photograph, admission decision/confirmation, proof of funds and origin if requested, accommodation evidence, all-risk health insurance, and civil-status documents where applicable. Police clearance may be requested; where required, it should generally be recent (OeAD says no older than three months at application).
- Foreign public documents must be legalised according to applicable rules and translated into German by a sworn/court-certified translator where required. Additional documents may be requested.
- After arriving and moving into accommodation, register the address with the local *Meldeamt* within **three working days**, subject to statutory exceptions.

## 9. Fees (2026)
- OeAD and the Embassy's 2026 residence page list the initial temporary residence permit card fee as **EUR 218**, payable at application and non-refundable if refused or withdrawn.
- This is not the same as any Visa D fee or external document, legalisation, translation, insurance, travel or VFS service cost. Confirm Visa D fee/payment instructions directly with the Embassy before filing.
- Some older pages still show EUR 120; do not use that old amount for a 2026 residence permit card application where the current fee schedule states EUR 218.

## 10. Counsellor verification checklist
1. Confirm nationality, actual residence, age on application, intake and programme duration.
2. Confirm institution and course eligibility; language-only courses do not qualify under the 40-ECTS non-degree route.
3. Confirm admission/conditional admission and any entrance exam pathway.
4. Calculate 2026 funds by age; include accommodation excess, insurance and regular financial obligations; verify funds are accessible and source documented.
5. Explain that Sri Lankan applicants submit the >6-month residence permit application in person at Austrian Embassy New Delhi and await decision abroad.
6. Separate EUR 218 residence card application fee from any Visa D fee and third-party/document costs.
7. Explain employer must secure AMS work authorisation before work begins; up to 20 hours/week may be granted without labour-market test.
8. Explain renewal study-progress conditions and conditional 12-month graduate job-search route.
9. Keep every rule Pending human review until authorised company reviewer signs off.
