# Liechtenstein — Student residence / visa source review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Status:** Pending human review — not approved for client legal advice  
**Scope:** Sri Lankan third-country national considering study at the University of Liechtenstein or another recognised Liechtenstein institution.

## Executive assessment
Liechtenstein is a highly restrictive and small study destination. Do not market it as a routine student-work migration route. The national Migration and Passport Office states that non-EEA/non-Swiss nationals may be granted a short-term study permit under Article 19 of the Foreign Nationals Act, subject to strict conditions. Employment is not automatic: the official student permit page says non-EEA students at the University of Liechtenstein may work up to 35% only where the work is related to their studies, and authorisation is required in every case. The university says full-time work is allowed during semester breaks, but the exact scope should be reconciled with the permit and written approval before advising a client.

## 1. Eligibility and admission
- Obtain written admission/confirmation from a recognised educational institution. Article 19 requires confirmation from the school management that the education/training can be commenced or continued.
- The applicant must have the language skills required for the course and a known expected duration of study.
- The University of Liechtenstein publishes programme-specific admission requirements and deadlines. For applicants requiring a visa, its published deadlines are earlier than those for EU/EEA/Swiss applicants; confirm the exact programme and intake deadline directly with the university.
- Do not assume every private course, language course or short course qualifies. Obtain written confirmation from the Migration and Passport Office that the programme qualifies for the study permit.

## 2. Residence permit and entry visa sequence
- For non-EEA/non-Swiss students, the relevant route is the short-term residence permit for study/training (L permit), under Article 19 of the Foreign Nationals Act.
- The L permit is issued for the duration of education, with a maximum validity of 12 months at a time. A further L permit may be applied for before expiry if the education is not completed.
- A complete application must reach the Migration and Passport Office no later than 14 days before the planned start of training. Visa-required applicants are advised to apply earlier.
- The applicant must not reside or work in Liechtenstein until the permit has been granted and the required entry visa has been issued. Third-country nationals must wait abroad for assurance/authorisation to issue the visa.
- Liechtenstein generally follows Swiss entry/visa rules. The Liechtenstein authority directs visa-required applicants to the Swiss representation responsible for their place of residence.

## 3. Financial means, accommodation and insurance
Article 19 of the Foreign Nationals Act requires:
- sufficient financial resources for living and study without recourse to social assistance;
- statutory health insurance covering all risks in Liechtenstein;
- suitable accommodation;
- a clean criminal-record position as required by law; and
- credible assurance of departure after the permitted stay.
No universal student-specific monthly maintenance figure was found on the current Liechtenstein Migration and Passport Office study-permit page. Do not import the short-stay border reference amount (CHF 30/day for a student) as the long-stay student residence threshold. Request written confirmation of the evidence and amount accepted for the individual application. The University of Liechtenstein currently says it provides a bank guarantee for third-country students and requires a CHF 3,000 deposit; verify applicability, refund terms and whether this applies to the applicant's programme before quoting it as a mandatory government fee.

## 4. Work during study
- The Migration and Passport Office says non-EEA/non-Swiss nationals who are students at the University of Liechtenstein may work up to 35% of a full-time workload, provided the activity is related to their studies.
- Employment is subject to prior authorisation in all cases. The university says the employer must submit a formal employment-approval request and the student may start only after official approval.
- The University states full-time employment is allowed during semester breaks. Because the authority's student permit page does not spell out this semester-break exception in the same detail, obtain written confirmation for the exact permit and dates before advising.
- This is not an open work right and should not be advertised as guaranteed part-time employment.

## 5. Post-study and dependants
The reviewed Liechtenstein student-permit guidance does not establish a general graduate job-search permit or automatic post-study work route for international students. Do not promise a post-study stay; obtain an individual written ruling from the Migration and Passport Office if a graduate wishes to remain.
Family reunification rules for third-country nationals are restrictive and are not established as an automatic student dependant entitlement by the reviewed student guidance. Do not promise spouse/children permits without a separate eligibility assessment and written authority confirmation.

## 6. Sri Lankan application route
- The Swiss Embassy in Colombo publishes a dedicated national student visa (D) checklist for Switzerland and Liechtenstein.
- The checklist requires an appointment and in-person appearance, three long-stay visa application forms, four identical colour photographs, valid and previous passports where available, and two sets of A4 photocopies in checklist order. Sinhala/Tamil documents require official translation into English, German, French or Italian.
- Contact the Embassy directly to confirm the current appointment, fee, supporting-document checklist and whether the Liechtenstein Migration and Passport Office has already issued the necessary assurance. Do not treat a Schengen short-stay C-visa process as the student residence route.

## 7. Counsellor document checklist
1. Passport and previous passport/visa history.
2. Official admission letter and programme details, study duration, full-time status and institution recognition.
3. Academic certificates/transcripts and certified translations where needed.
4. Evidence of required language proficiency.
5. Evidence of sufficient funds for tuition and living costs; confirm acceptable source and format with the authority.
6. Confirmed suitable accommodation.
7. Health insurance covering all risks in Liechtenstein.
8. Police clearance/criminal-record evidence as requested.
9. Study plan, motivation and credible departure plan where requested.
10. Completed national D-visa forms, photographs, originals and two A4 copy sets per Embassy checklist.
11. Any university CHF 3,000 deposit/bank-guarantee agreement, after verifying the terms.

## 8. Critical cautions / unresolved points
- No official fixed monthly student subsistence figure located on the current study-permit page; request written confirmation.
- CHF 30/day is a short-stay border reference amount and must not be represented as a long-term student permit requirement.
- Work is not automatic; 35% is subject to study-related activity and prior authorisation. Confirm semester-break work in writing.
- No general post-study job-search route confirmed.
- University CHF 3,000 deposit is a university bank-guarantee arrangement, not a government visa fee.
- Confirm whether the applicant's specific programme and institution qualify under Article 19.
- All legal interpretations remain Pending human review.
