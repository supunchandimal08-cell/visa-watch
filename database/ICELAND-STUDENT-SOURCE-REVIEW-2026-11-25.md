# Iceland — Student Immigration Source Review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Country:** Iceland  
**Status:** Pending human review  
**Applicant scope:** Sri Lankan / non-EEA/EFTA applicant applying for full-time higher education.

## Critical 2026 change
Iceland enacted important changes effective **8 July 2026**. Older university and third-party pages may still describe the previous requirement for a separate student work permit, a 22.5-hour work limit, a three-year post-study period, or academic progress only at first renewal. Do not rely on those old statements. The Directorate of Immigration's 8 July 2026 notice is the controlling current public explanation.

## Standard route
1. Obtain admission to an eligible Icelandic university programme.
2. Apply for the student residence permit directly to the Directorate of Immigration in Iceland. The application and supporting documents must be received as original paper documents; the current page gives 1 May for fall semester and 1 October for spring semester.
3. Sri Lankan nationals require an entry visa. After residence permit approval, the Ministry sends a request for an entry D visa to the designated embassy. The official embassy list names the Royal Norwegian Embassy / VFS Global in Colombo for Sri Lanka.
4. Travel only after the required approval/entry visa. Complete photo appointment, residence notification and any required medical examination after arrival.

## Eligibility and admission
- General student applicants must be at least 18.
- Full-time university study means 30 ECTS per semester.
- Other specified routes include qualifying doctoral collaboration, recognised exchange programmes, internships forming part of studies (with separate work permit conditions), and recognised technical/workplace study.
- Individual universities control admission, English-language evidence, tuition and deposits.

## Financial means
The Directorate's current student requirements publish:
- One individual: **ISK 259,951 per month**
- Married couple: **ISK 415,922 per month**
- Additional family member aged 18+: **ISK 129,976 per month**

Funds must cover the entire intended residence-permit period. The student must demonstrate independent financial means. The only stated third-party exception is a legally resident marital spouse with sufficient resources for both. Cash, property and uncertain investment income are not accepted as secure support. Recheck the live amount before lodgement.

## Health insurance and documents
- Insurance must be valid in Iceland for at least three months from registration of legal domicile and have minimum cover of **ISK 2,000,000**.
- Submit criminal-record certificate, passport, passport photo, admission confirmation, proof of funds, insurance and proof of processing-fee payment.
- A medical examination may be required after arrival depending on nationality.
- Confirm document age, translation, apostille/legalisation and insurance wording for the specific applicant.

## Permit duration and renewal
A student residence permit may be granted for up to one year at a time and renewed for up to one year at a time while eligibility and academic progress continue. Since 8 July 2026, satisfactory academic progress must be shown at **every renewal**: at least **75% of a full-time course load during the academic year**. Staying abroad for more than three consecutive months or registering domicile abroad may affect/revoke the permit. Confirm exact filing deadline and current processing instructions.

## Work rights — updated 8 July 2026
- Valid student residence permit holders may work as employees without a separate work permit up to **60% of full-time employment** in total across all employers.
- During scheduled academic breaks, including summer and Christmas holidays, students may work full-time.
- Self-employment is prohibited.
- First-time applicants may not work until the student residence permit has been issued.
- Employment details must be provided at renewal; exceeding the permitted limit may jeopardise work rights.
- Do not convert 60% to a weekly hour number unless the current legal full-time baseline is confirmed.

## Post-study
A graduate who completes a Bachelor's, Master's or Doctoral degree at an Icelandic university may renew the student residence permit for up to **18 months** after graduation to seek employment based on specialist qualifications. A doctoral graduate may also qualify for a specialist job-seeking permit for up to 12 months. These are separate residence bases, not automatic unrestricted work permission or a job guarantee.

## Dependants
After the 8 July 2026 amendments, family reunification is limited to students enrolled in full-time university programmes leading to Bachelor's, Master's or Doctoral degrees. Eligible family members include spouse, qualifying cohabiting partner (at least one year cohabitation), and children under 18 where the student has custody. Diploma students and parents are not eligible under this student route. Family applications are not processed simultaneously with the student's application.

## Fees and Sri Lanka route
- Student residence-permit processing fee shown on the Directorate page: **ISK 70,000**, payable by bank transfer; non-refundable.
- Sri Lanka entry D visa: official Iceland MFA list shows **Colombo — Royal Norwegian Embassy / VFS Global**. D visa is requested after residence permit approval.
- Do not confuse this with the short-stay Schengen C visa route. Confirm D-visa appointment, current fee and VFS handling charge directly with the Norwegian Embassy/VFS before payment.
