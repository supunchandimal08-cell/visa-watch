# UAE / Dubai Student Residence and Work-Rights Review — 25 November 2026

## Scope and review status
This review is for non-UAE national applicants applying from Sri Lanka to study in the UAE, with Dubai-specific GDRFA services distinguished from federal ICP rules. UAE student status is a **residence permit / residence visa**, not a Schengen or European student visa. All dashboard rules remain **Pending human review**. The sponsor, emirate, institution, programme, applicant's current UAE status and live service channel can change the applicable process and charges.

## 1. Student residence route and sponsor
- The UAE Government states that a student may stay for study under sponsorship of a UAE-resident parent or the accredited university/college where the student studies.
- For a foreign student enrolled in an accredited university, college, educational institution or research institute, GDRFA Dubai describes the education institution as the student's host and guarantor.
- A student residence permit does not itself require the student to work.
- The UAE Government says the student application requires a certificate from the university/institute specifying the duration of study.
- Confirm the exact sponsor in writing before payment: institution-sponsored and parent-sponsored applications are not interchangeable. For a Dubai case use GDRFA Dubai; for other emirates use the relevant ICP/federal or emirate channel.

## 2. Institution and course checks
- GDRFA Dubai's service conditions require the university/college/institute/research centre to be licensed to operate in the UAE and to have approval from the Ministry of Education or the competent educational authority in the relevant emirate, as applicable.
- For Dubai, check the institution in the KHDA higher-education directory and verify the specific programme, campus, award and current regulator status directly with KHDA and the institution.
- A KHDA directory listing alone is not a student visa approval and does not replace GDRFA/ICP eligibility or sponsor confirmation.

## 3. GDRFA Dubai student residence permit — core documents
The current GDRFA Dubai service page lists:
- Recent colour personal photograph with white background.
- Passport copy.
- Medical examination approved by the concerned authorities.
- Emirates ID application receipt.
- Health insurance.
The page also states institutional licensing and competent education-authority approval conditions. The institution/Amer/GDRFA may require additional sponsor or case-specific documents. Check the live service checklist before submission; do not treat this list as exhaustive.

## 4. Fees and processing estimate
The GDRFA Dubai student residence issuance service currently lists:
- Residence permit fee: AED 200.
- Knowledge Dirham: AED 10.
- Innovation Dirham: AED 10.
- Inside-country charge: AED 500.
- Delivery: AED 20.
- It notes an additional AED 100 annually whenever residence is for more than two years.
- Expected completion time is shown as 48 hours after requirements are complete.
- The page states a 60-day grace period after expiry or cancellation for the service described.
These are service-page line items, not a guaranteed universal total. AED 740 is only the arithmetic sum if every listed charge applies. Confirm the applicant's route, inside/outside status, duration, medical/Emirates ID/insurance charges, institution administration fees and any current changes with GDRFA/Amer. The 48-hour figure is an estimate, not a promise.

## 5. Work rights — do not promise automatic part-time work
- A student residence visa is not, by itself, proof of permission to take any job.
- UAE Government / MoHRE guidance says a person must hold the appropriate valid work permit before working; working without a valid work permit is illegal.
- The federal employment framework includes a specific student training and employment permit category for establishments registered with MoHRE. The permit application is employer-led and subject to eligibility and conditions.
- GDRFA Dubai's student FAQ describes internships in line with academic requirements. That FAQ should not be interpreted as blanket permission for any paid employment.
- Before a student accepts a job or internship, verify the exact permit type, employer's MoHRE eligibility, student's age/status, institution's written approval where relevant, and whether the activity is course-integral or ordinary employment. Do not state a general 20-hour or 40-hour student work limit unless an applicable official rule for that case expressly sets one.
- Never rely on future part-time earnings as the student's primary evidence of tuition or living-cost funds; never promise job placement, guaranteed hours or income.

## 6. Parent sponsorship and family points
- The UAE Government's student residence page says an expatriate resident may sponsor male children up to age 25; male students over 25 may remain on parent sponsorship if evidence of attendance at a higher education institution is provided. Female children may continue under the parent's sponsorship regardless of age until married.
- These are parent-sponsored dependent residence provisions, not a blanket student visa age limit. Confirm current sponsor eligibility, relationship documents, insurance, accommodation and emirate-specific procedure with ICP/GDRFA.
- The federal portal also describes residence for family members of foreign students at UAE universities where suitable housing is affordable. Do not promise dependant permission without checking the student's programme, sponsor, housing and current authority process.

## 7. After graduation — distinguish Golden Residence from ordinary student renewal
- ICP provides a service for graduates of accredited universities within the UAE to complete procedures for a 10-year Golden Residence.
- The ICP service page lists valid UAE health insurance, passport validity of at least six months, and a university classified A or B under the Ministry of Education's accreditation rating. It asks for passport, photo and a Bachelor's, Master's or Doctorate degree from a university accredited and licensed by the Ministry.
- This is a specific eligibility-based Golden Residence route, not an automatic post-study work visa for every graduate. Check the current classification, degree, application category and all ICP conditions. Do not tell every graduate they receive 10 years or unrestricted work rights automatically.

## 8. Sri Lanka applicant workflow
1. Confirm whether the applicant is applying from Sri Lanka or already inside the UAE, and record the current immigration status.
2. Obtain a written unconditional admission/offer and exact fee schedule, refund terms and course dates.
3. Verify the institution and programme with the correct emirate education regulator; for Dubai use KHDA and check the actual campus/course.
4. Obtain written confirmation of the visa sponsor (institution or eligible UAE-resident parent), who submits the application, included charges and refund policy if refused.
5. Collect the sponsor's current document checklist. Common GDRFA documents include passport copy, photo, approved medical, Emirates ID receipt and health insurance; extra documents may apply.
6. Confirm whether the student needs an entry permit before travel or a status-change procedure inside UAE. Do not advise travel on a visit visa as a guaranteed conversion route.
7. Confirm the authority's application reference, entry permission, medical, Emirates ID and residence issuance steps; do not treat an admission letter or application receipt as residence approval.
8. Explain work permit requirements separately from student residence status and do not promise a job.
9. Give the student copies of the issued permit, insurance, Emirates ID application/receipt, sponsor contacts and expiry/renewal dates.
10. Record official URLs, access date, fees quoted, responsible reviewer and any unresolved exception in the client file using the company's approved secure system.
