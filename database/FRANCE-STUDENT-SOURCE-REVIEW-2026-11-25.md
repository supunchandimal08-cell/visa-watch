# France Student Visa — Official Source Review (25 November 2026)

## Scope and status
This is a counsellor reference for applicants residing in Sri Lanka who plan higher education in France. It is not legal advice and does not predict visa outcomes. All dashboard rules remain **Pending human review** until an authorised reviewer checks and approves them.

## 1. Correct visa route
- France-Visas says applicants must be over 18, have chosen their course and be accepted by a higher-education institution.
- For a course longer than three months, the relevant route is generally a long-stay student visa; the exact visa wording and duration must be checked.
- The French government lists the countries covered by the Études en France (EEF) procedure. Sri Lanka is not on the published list. A Sri Lanka-resident applicant should therefore start with the France-Visas visa assistant and the Sri Lanka country page, and follow the route shown for their nationality/residence. Do not tell a Sri Lankan applicant that EEF is mandatory without a current official instruction.
- Admission/pre-enrolment is not a visa guarantee.

## 2. Financial resources
Service-Public, verified 1 August 2026, states at least **€877.50 per month** for the relevant non-EU student residence category. It also describes evidence such as scholarship documents, recent payslips, bank balance evidence and third-party support with identity and regular-transfer evidence. The resource condition has exceptions, including certain successful competitive-entry cases. Check nationality, legal category, evidence type, tuition and accommodation separately. Do not represent €877.50 as a complete all-in budget or as an unconditional rule for every applicant.

## 3. Work during studies
France-Visas states that foreign students are generally allowed to work up to **964 hours per year** (60% of normal annual working time). Algerian nationals have a different 50% limit. Work is supplementary income and should not be presented as a way to guarantee tuition or living costs.

## 4. VLS-TS after arrival
If the issued document is a VLS-TS étudiant, the student must validate it online within **three months** after arrival. Service-Public currently lists a **€100** validation tax. Confirm the visa wording, current online procedure and fee; other long-stay visa categories can have different formalities.

## 5. Renewal and study progress
For the student residence status described by Service-Public, the renewal application is made online no earlier than four months and no later than two months before expiry. Evidence includes enrolment/pre-enrolment, academic progress and sufficient resources. Authorities assess the real and serious nature of studies, including attendance and results. The student's exact permit and current online instructions control.

## 6. Sri Lanka application channel and timing
- The France-Visas Sri Lanka page says applications are processed by the Embassy of France in Colombo, the only authority authorised to decide.
- VFS Global in Colombo 9 receives applications.
- The page states normal processing is 15 calendar days from a complete and admissible application; this can extend up to 60 calendar days where investigation or additional documents are needed.
- The page lists a basic VFS service fee of €19.48, collected in local currency using the Embassy exchange rate. Visa application fees are non-refundable.
- Appointment availability, exact visa fee, exchange rate, supporting documents and current processing estimates must be checked live before advising. Never promise a decision date.

## Counsellor checklist
1. Confirm applicant nationality, legal residence in Sri Lanka, age, intended course duration and exact institution/programme.
2. Verify institution acceptance and course start date; confirm the correct visa type using France-Visas.
3. Use the current Sri Lanka country page for submission jurisdiction, VFS appointment and fees.
4. Assess the financial plan separately: tuition, accommodation, living costs, travel, sponsor income/source of funds and acceptable evidence.
5. Check language requirements and the institution's own admission conditions; do not invent a universal French-language requirement.
6. Explain the 964-hour general work limit and that a job/income is not guaranteed.
7. Before travel, explain the VLS-TS three-month validation deadline only if that is the visa issued.
8. Keep every legal rule Pending human review until reviewer name, date, source and approval are recorded.
