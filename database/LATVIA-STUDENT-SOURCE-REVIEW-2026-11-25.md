# Latvia — Student immigration source review

**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Country:** Latvia 🇱🇻  
**Audience:** VisaWatch counsellors and reviewers  
**Status:** Pending human review — not approved for client legal advice

## Executive summary

A Sri Lankan non-EU student normally needs admission to an accredited Latvian higher-education institution, a university-filed OCMA invitation, a national long-stay D visa, and then a Latvian temporary residence permit after arrival. The Embassy of Latvia in New Delhi publishes Sri Lanka-specific instructions directing applicants to a Hello Verify check and VFS Colombo appointment. The student should not be advised to travel on a short-stay C visa as a substitute for the study route.

## Main rules and evidence

1. **Admission and route:** accredited institution and study agreement; institution submits invitation to OCMA. Sri Lanka-specific D-visa process via New Delhi mission / VFS Colombo and Hello Verify. D visa is stated as 90 days, followed by residence-permit application in Latvia.
2. **Residence validity:** EU Immigration Portal says permit period follows the study agreement plus four months; residence card is issued for one year and renewed/registered annually. Academic progress matters; repeated interruption or excessive delay can lead to refusal.
3. **Funds:** PMLP current general subsistence table (updated 16 March 2026; effective 1 April 2026) says EUR 780/month for cases not otherwise specified and EUR 234/month for a minor child. PMLP's older study page still says EUR 620/month and an older graduate page EUR 500/month. This is a material official-source conflict. Use EUR 780/month as a cautious planning figure only, not a confirmed student-specific threshold, until PMLP confirms the correct legal basis and required period in writing. Tuition/accommodation are additional unless accepted otherwise.
4. **Work:** EU Immigration Portal says 20 hours/week during studies and 40 hours/week during holidays, with graduate/postgraduate students able to work 40 hours/week. Study in Latvia specifically states Bachelor's 20 hours/week and Master's/doctoral full-time work during study breaks. Resolve Master's/doctoral term-time limits against current law and permit annotation before counselling.
5. **Post-study:** full-time Bachelor's students are described as receiving four additional months after studies. Full-time Master's/doctoral students receive four months and may request a further nine months for job search; the extension is discretionary and must be applied for before expiry. Not an automatic nine months for all graduates.
6. **Insurance:** mandatory for the planned stay; Study in Latvia states EUR 42,600 minimum coverage for a long-stay visa, including emergency care, inpatient treatment and repatriation.
7. **Documents / fees:** EU portal lists passport, application, photo, study agreement, subsistence evidence, criminal-record certificate where applicable, and approved university invitation. Its indicative fees are EUR 60 visa and EUR 70 / 200 / 400 residence processing tiers; confirm current PMLP tariff, Master's/doctoral exemption, VFS/Hello Verify charges and all ancillary costs.
8. **Sri Lanka route:** Embassy New Delhi student instructions specifically mention Sri Lankan nationals and VFS Colombo. However, the URL redirected during this research to a temporary MFA site notice. Reconfirm current appointment and document verification requirements directly with Embassy/VFS.

## Counsellor workflow / stop points

- Verify institution accreditation, exact programme, full-time status, campus, tuition and study agreement.
- Obtain written confirmation of the OCMA invitation number and the current Sri Lankan D-visa route.
- Complete Hello Verify only after confirming the currently authorised provider and fee.
- Obtain current PMLP confirmation of the student maintenance amount; do not rely on stale EUR 620/EUR 500 values.
- Confirm work rights by programme level and permit annotation; do not promise employment or self-funding through part-time work.
- Confirm insurance coverage of at least EUR 42,600 for the long-stay visa and full intended period.
- Confirm current visa / residence fees, criminal record, translations, legalisation, medical/X-ray, arrival declaration and renewal steps.
- Keep all legal interpretations marked **Pending human review** until a named reviewer checks the primary legislation and current authority instructions.
