# Canada Student Permit — Official Source Review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Scope:** Sri Lankan applicants seeking a Canadian study permit, including DLI/PAL checks, funds, work during studies, placements, and PGWP.  
**Status:** Research update only. All VisaWatch rules remain **Pending human review**.

## Key current requirements

### 1. Admission, DLI and PAL/TAL
- Verify the exact institution and campus on IRCC's Designated Learning Institution list. DLI status alone does not establish PGWP eligibility; check the exact programme separately.
- A letter of acceptance from the DLI is normally required.
- A PAL/TAL is generally required for post-secondary study and should be submitted with the study permit application.
- From 1 January 2026, applicants to degree-granting master's or doctoral programmes at a public DLI are exempt from PAL/TAL. Other exemptions include certain school levels, qualifying Quebec vocational programmes, exchanges without tuition to the DLI, specified Global Affairs Canada scholarships and certain in-Canada cases. Evidence of exemption must be uploaded.
- PAL/TAL is linked to the school, normally valid for its 2026 cap year until 31 December 2026 unless an earlier expiry is printed. Changing school or level may require a new letter.
- Quebec has its own CAQ / provincial financial process; do not apply the non-Quebec funds table to Quebec.

### 2. Financial support — effective 1 September 2026
IRCC requires funds without relying on work in Canada for tuition, living expenses for applicant and accompanying family, and travel to/from Canada. Outside Quebec, for applications received on or after 1 September 2026, annual living funds are:
| Family size | Annual living funds (CAD) |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 23,448 |
| 2 | 29,192 |
| 3 | 35,888 |
| 4 | 43,572 |
| 5 | 49,419 |
| 6 | 55,736 |
| 7 | 62,054 |
| Each additional person | +6,318 |
These amounts exclude tuition and transport. For a programme longer than one year, explain how the full programme will be funded. Common evidence includes six months of bank statements with source of funds, education loan, scholarship, paid tuition/housing receipt plus remaining funds, or sponsor letter with relationship, employment/income and capacity evidence. The current IRCC Sri Lanka checklist IMM 5836 E (02-2026) requests six months of applicant/spouse/parents or supporter bank records, fixed deposits and income evidence as relevant.

### 3. Application and arrival
- Study permit applications are generally submitted online before travelling to Canada.
- Current IRCC fee list: study permit CAD 150. Biometrics fee is generally CAD 85 per individual, subject to exemptions and fee changes.
- Most applicants aged 14–79 need biometrics unless exempt; follow the Biometric Instruction Letter and book the appointment with the Colombo VAC.
- Colombo Canada VAC: No. 675, Dr Danister De Silva Mawatha, Colombo 09. VAC appointment and services should be checked live.
- Approval letter / letter of introduction is not the study permit itself. Present it and any required TRV at the port of entry; the border officer makes the final entry decision and issues the study permit if admitted.
- Do not promise a processing time. Check IRCC live processing times and the personalised document checklist at the time of filing.

### 4. Work during studies
Eligible students may work off campus up to 24 hours per week during regular academic sessions and unlimited hours during scheduled breaks, only if all IRCC conditions are met. Usually this means full-time enrolment at a DLI in a qualifying post-secondary academic/vocational/professional programme of at least six months leading to a degree, diploma or certificate; studies have started; valid study permit contains the work condition; and the student has a SIN. Narrow final-semester part-time exception may apply. Students on authorised leave, between schools and not studying, or enrolled only in ESL/FSL, general-interest or prerequisite-only courses cannot rely on this off-campus work authorisation. Never promise work or use future earnings as proof of funds.

### 5. Mandatory co-op / placement
IRCC states that from 1 April 2026 eligible post-secondary international students do not need a separate co-op work permit for a qualifying placement required to complete the programme and approved by the DLI. This is not blanket permission for any internship. Secondary-level students still require a co-op work permit. Obtain written DLI confirmation and check the exact current exception before placement.

### 6. Post-graduation work permit (PGWP)
PGWP is not automatic. Check exact DLI and programme eligibility, credential, programme length, study mode, full-time history, transfers/leaves, language and field-of-study rules, and application date. General criteria include eligible programme/DLI, usually at least eight months (or 900 hours for Quebec programmes), required full-time status with limited exceptions, and application within 180 days after completion confirmation. Language thresholds and field-of-study requirements vary by credential and application date. Bachelor's, master's and doctoral graduates generally require CLB/NCLC 7 in all four abilities and have no field-of-study requirement; other university graduates generally CLB/NCLC 7; college/polytechnic/non-university graduates generally CLB/NCLC 5. Certain non-degree programmes may have field-of-study requirements where study permit application was submitted on/after 1 November 2024. Always verify current IRCC eligibility page and DLI programme list.

## Human reviewer checklist
- Confirm current IRCC DLI and exact programme PGWP eligibility before enrolment advice.
- Verify PAL/TAL requirement or documented exemption for the applicant's course level, public/private DLI, province, start date and application date.
- Recalculate funds from the live IRCC table for family size and application date; add actual tuition and travel; assess source/access and entire programme funding.
- Use the applicant's personalised IRCC checklist and current Sri Lanka IMM 5836 version; local instructions can change.
- Confirm biometrics exemption/status and book Colombo VAC only after the IRCC instruction.
- Check study permit conditions, SIN, full-time status and course category before telling a student they can work.
- Confirm mandatory co-op placement and DLI approval before work begins.
- Recheck current PGWP rules against the exact credential and study permit application date.
- No rule is legally approved by this research note. Dashboard rules remain Pending human review.
