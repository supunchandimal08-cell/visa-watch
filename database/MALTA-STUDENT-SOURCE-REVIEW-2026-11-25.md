# Malta — Student immigration source review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Status:** Pending human review — not approved for client legal advice  
**Scope:** Sri Lankan third-country national considering full-time higher education in Malta.

## Executive summary
For a course longer than 90 days, a third-country student needs a National Long-Stay D visa for education. Sri Lankan residents can submit the student long-stay application through the Malta VFS centre in Colombo. The study residence permit route is primarily for full-time programmes at licensed institutions leading to an MQRIC-recognised MQF Level 5+ higher-education qualification. Student work is not automatic: the relevant Jobsplus employment licence must be issued first. Identità's checklist calculates funds as at least 75% of the national minimum wage for every month of study. Eligible MQF 5+ graduates may apply once for a 9-month post-study stay, but that permit itself is not permission to start full-time work.

## 1. Course eligibility and institution checks
- Identità's Expatriates Unit says the study residence permit regime under S.L. 217.22 applies to third-country nationals accepted by an institution licensed by the National Commission for Further and Higher Education (NCFHE/MFHEA) for a full-time course leading to a higher-education qualification recognised by MQRIC at MQF Level 5 or above.
- Preparatory programme may qualify where it leads to the higher-education qualification.
- Check the institution's current licence and exact course listing, MQF level, full-time status, ECTS, course length, contact hours and campus. Do not assume every private college, English course or foundation programme is eligible for the MQF5+ residence permit.
- Courses at MQF 1–4 or language courses may have different visa and work conditions. Verify exact course classification with Identità before advising.

## 2. Visa route and Sri Lanka submission
- Courses exceeding 90 calendar days: National Long-Stay D visa for education.
- Courses not exceeding 90 days: normally Schengen C visa; do not mix this with the long-stay student route.
- VFS Malta Sri Lanka's current extended-services page includes a Long Stay Visa > Student category and routes applications to the Central Visa Unit within Identità.
- VFS Malta Sri Lanka page lists student visa Standard EUR100 and VFS service fee EUR100; Extended EUR150 and VFS service fee EUR150. Payments are collected in local currency at current exchange rate; check live appointment/payment page.
- The VFS page states long-stay processing takes a minimum 6–8 weeks. Identità checklist asks applications to be submitted at least 9 weeks before course start. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed decisions.
- Incomplete, unclear or unsigned applications may not be accepted at counter.

## 3. Residence permit after arrival
- MQF Level 5+ full-time higher-education students apply for a study residence permit through Identità Expatriates Unit Portal; the applicant must complete the required in-person steps in Malta.
- Identità lists EUR50 as the study/research/training application fee on its Expatriates Unit page.
- The National Long-Stay D visa is entry permission; it is not a substitute for the student residence card where the residence permit route applies.
- Renewal: Servizz.gov.mt says applicants should ideally apply around 3 months before current permit expiry. Some Identità student visa extension procedures require contacting CVU 30 to 21 days before expiry with satisfactory performance and attendance evidence. The correct process depends on course category and visa/permit status; check the live portal instructions.

## 4. Funds and sponsorship
- Identità's Student National Visa checklist requires last 3 months of bank statements plus a recent bank certificate. Funds must amount to at least 75% of the national minimum wage for every month of study.
- If sponsored: sponsor's last 3 months bank statements, signed declaration and copy of sponsor's ID (front/back) or passport bio page.
- Malta's DIER lists 2026 adult national minimum wage at EUR229.44 per week. Annualised monthly equivalent = EUR229.44 × 52 ÷ 12 = EUR994.24. 75% indicative monthly amount = EUR745.68.
- The EUR745.68 is an arithmetic estimate from the published weekly rate, not an independent fixed visa amount. Confirm Identità's exact month-count/calculation, sponsor requirements and whether tuition/accommodation evidence is also required for the individual file.
- This visa minimum is not a realistic full living-cost budget. Counsellors should prepare a separate budget including rent, food, transport, utilities, books, tuition and contingency.

## 5. Insurance and documents
Identità's published Student National Visa checklist includes:
1. Completed and signed visa application.
2. Passport valid for at least 10 months.
3. One ICAO-standard passport photograph.
4. VFS appointment letter.
5. Enrolment letter showing institution name as registered with MFHEA, exact course title as listed, MQF level, ECTS, duration, full/part-time mode, course breaks, weekly contact hours and teaching address.
6. Proof of tuition payment (receipt or invoice).
7. Applicant's 3-month bank statements and recent bank certificate; sponsor documents if relevant.
8. Copy of international bank card to access funds in Malta.
9. Medical/travel insurance:
   - MQF 5+: Schengen-valid, at least EUR30,000 medical cover and minimum 91 consecutive days from first Schengen entry, study purpose.
   - MQF 4 and below: Schengen-valid, EUR30,000 minimum cover for the entire stay.
10. Any additional current VFS/Identità checklist items, originals and certified translations as instructed.
The checklist PDF was published August 2025; recheck for a newer version before accepting documents. Students at University of Malta, MCAST and Institute of Tourism Studies are listed by Identità as exempt from the health insurance requirement for the study residence permit, but the visa-stage insurance requirement may still apply; confirm with CVU/VFS for the individual route.

## 6. Work during studies
- Identità's Central Visa Unit employment page says third-country nationals pursuing a full-time course leading to a MQRIC-recognised higher-education qualification may work up to 20 hours per week from their first year, after residence permit issuance and with the relevant Jobsplus work permit/licence.
- Identità says offers related to the student's chosen study area may be viewed more favourably.
- For other study courses exceeding 90 days that do not qualify under S.L. 217.22, work may be allowed only after the first 3 months, subject to Jobsplus conditions and sector restrictions.
- Never advise the student to start working before the licence is issued. Work rights are conditional and do not guarantee a job.
- The older EU Immigration Portal page also describes 20h/week and separate rules for MQF 1–4; current Identità guidance should be primary.

## 7. Attendance, progress and renewal
- Identità's student obligations document requires regular class attendance and sufficient academic progress. Failure may result in withdrawal of residence authorisation after consultation with the host institution.
- Employment without a Jobsplus licence is prohibited.
- Keep attendance records, academic results, progression evidence, tuition payment records and updated accommodation/insurance.
- Apply for renewal well before expiry; general government service recommends about 3 months in advance, but follow the exact Identità route and deadlines.

## 8. Post-study
- Identità Study FAQ states non-EU students who successfully complete a course at MQF Level 5 or higher and held a valid study permit at completion may apply for a one-time permit for up to 9 months to seek employment or start a business related to their field.
- Must apply while legally in Malta and before student permit expires; Identità's FAQ references Form CEA N.03 and supporting documents including course completion proof, passport/permit, funds, insurance and accommodation.
- The 9-month permit is not itself a work permit. The graduate must secure the appropriate Jobsplus/Single Permit or other work authorisation before starting full-time work.
- Do not promise a job, automatic conversion, or that the 9-month period is renewable.

## 9. Counsellor risk flags
- Verify institution licence and exact course MQF level; MQF 5+ is central to the student residence/work/post-study route.
- Distinguish C visa (up to 90 days) and D visa (over 90 days).
- Funds formula: 75% of national minimum wage per month. 2026 indicative calculation EUR745.68/month; confirm with authority.
- Student work requires Jobsplus licence; 20h/week applies to qualifying higher-education students; other courses have a different rule.
- Post-study is one-time 9 months for eligible MQF5+ graduates; not a work permit.
- Attendance and academic progress are immigration compliance conditions.
- VFS checklist PDF dates from August 2025; check whether updated before file submission.
