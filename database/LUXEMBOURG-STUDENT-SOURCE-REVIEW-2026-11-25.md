# Luxembourg — Student immigration source review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Status:** Pending human review — not approved for client legal advice  
**Scope:** Sri Lankan third-country national seeking full-time higher education in Luxembourg.

## Executive summary
Luxembourg changed its first-residence procedure on 1 October 2026. New third-country applicants must apply for a long-stay Visa D through the competent Luxembourg or Belgian diplomatic/consular post, depending on jurisdiction. The former temporary authorisation to stay (AST)-first process continues only for applications lodged on or before 30 September 2026 under transitional provisions. For Sri Lankan residents, the Luxembourg Embassy in New Delhi handles long-stay D visas; the application must be couriered through the Honorary Consulate of Luxembourg in Colombo. Do not confuse this with short-stay Luxembourg visas, which are represented by the Swiss Embassy in Colombo.

## 1. Eligibility and institution
- Applicant must be admitted to a full-time higher-education cycle leading to a higher education degree/diploma at an eligible institution: University of Luxembourg, an institution awarding the BTS advanced technician certificate, or an approved private higher-education institution.
- Doctoral programme researchers are included in the student category.
- A short course, language course or part-time programme should not be assumed to qualify; obtain written confirmation from the institution and Immigration.
- The current page defines the student route as full-time study. Confirm admission, programme recognition, language and academic conditions directly with the institution.

## 2. Visa D process — 1 October 2026 change
- For a stay over 90 days, third-country nationals need a long-stay Visa D.
- Since 1 October 2026, new first-residence applicants submit the Visa D application to the competent Luxembourg or Belgian diplomatic/consular post, depending on jurisdiction; the old AST-first process is replaced.
- Applications submitted on or before 30 September 2026 continue under the former procedure; already-issued temporary authorisations remain valid according to their conditions.
- Visa D is normally issued for 6 months. After arrival, the student must declare arrival at the commune, complete the medical check-up and apply for the student residence permit before Visa D expires.
- The Visa D is entry permission; it is not the final student residence card.
- Current Guichet student page says complete-file response is in principle no more than 60 days, but incomplete files suspend the period. Do not promise a guaranteed processing time.

## 3. Sri Lankan application route
- Luxembourg Embassy New Delhi page (updated 23 November 2026) explicitly says Sri Lankan long-stay D visa applications are processed at the Embassy in New Delhi and must be couriered through the Honorary Consulate of Luxembourg in Sri Lanka, at 103/14 Dharmapala Mawatha, Colombo 7. Confirm current address, contact and courier method before sending.
- New Delhi page lists EUR 50 fee and suggests submitting at least 25–30 working days before travel to allow courier and processing. These are published instructions; verify current payment details and processing times.
- Luxembourg Government Sri Lanka mission page says long-stay visas are handled by Luxembourg Embassy New Delhi, while short-stay visa applications are handled by Swiss Embassy Colombo.
- Do not send a student D visa to the Swiss Embassy Colombo; it handles short-stay representation only.

## 4. Funds, sponsorship and insurance
- Student must show sufficient resources for living expenses throughout study and return travel.
- Monthly resources must be at least 80% of the REVIS social inclusion income currently in force.
- Evidence accepted may include: original scholarship/student-loan certificate with amount and duration; bank certificate in student's name; where parent-funded, bank evidence showing sufficient funds and parental solvency for at least 12 months covering living, healthcare and education plus return travel; or formal financial-support undertaking by a Luxembourg-resident guarantor.
- Material benefits such as free accommodation or an eligible employment contract of no more than 15 hours/week may be considered.
- REVIS is household-composition and income dependent. Official June 2026 data shows EUR 972.20 monthly adult inclusion allowance; 80% arithmetic reference is EUR 777.76. Do not quote EUR 777.76 as a definitive universal threshold without confirming the precise REVIS component used by Immigration for that application.
- Travel health insurance must cover the Schengen area for the entire visa period and include medical treatment, hospitalisation, repatriation for medical reasons and funeral costs.
- Documents not in English, French or German require certified translation.

## 5. Work during study
- Current Guichet.lu student Visa D page (updated 1 October 2026) authorises paid work up to an average of 15 hours per week per month outside study time.
- The 15-hour limit does not apply to paid work during school holidays, doctoral research at the higher-education institution/accredited research institute, or University of Luxembourg assistant contracts.
- Employer must submit a written declaration to the General Department of Immigration before hiring or changing the contract and notify the Department within 3 working days after work begins.
- Older EU Immigration Portal page (1 April 2025) says Bachelor/BTS students cannot work during their first two semesters, then 15h/week; it allows 40h/week during student holidays. This conflicts with the current Guichet page's more general 15-hour rule and holiday exception. Primary current Guichet page is used here, but the first-two-semester issue must be resolved by a human reviewer/Immigration before counselling first-year Bachelor students.
- No employment is guaranteed.

## 6. Post-study route
- Current Guichet page permits eligible students to apply for a 9-month residence permit to seek employment or create a business after successfully completing the final year of a Master's degree in Luxembourg or defending a doctoral thesis for research carried out in Luxembourg.
- Application must be submitted before the student permit expires.
- Evidence includes passport, recent Luxembourg criminal-record extract, Master's completion/doctoral thesis defence, comprehensive health insurance and sufficient resources at 80% of current REVIS plus return travel.
- The current official page does not extend this graduate job-search route to ordinary Bachelor's graduates. Do not promise it to Bachelor-only clients.
- Any subsequent employed/self-employed permit must be applied for before the 9-month permit expires and the work must relate to the student's academic education, subject to current law.

## 7. Dependants
The current student Visa D page does not itself establish an automatic spouse/child dependant entitlement. Family reunification is a separate residence category with its own legal and financial conditions. Do not promise student dependants without separate assessment and written confirmation from Immigration.

## 8. Counsellor document checklist
1. Full valid passport copy (all pages).
2. Admission proof for eligible full-time higher-education programme.
3. Criminal-record extract from current country of residence (adult applicants, issued within 3 months) or justified affidavit.
4. Evidence of funds: scholarship/loan, own bank certificate, parent bank and solvency evidence for 12 months, or formal Luxembourg guarantor undertaking.
5. Return-travel cost evidence.
6. Travel health insurance for entire Visa D period covering medical, hospitalisation, repatriation and funeral costs.
7. Parental authorisation if under 18.
8. Certified translation for documents not in English/French/German.
9. New Delhi Embassy's application form, photograph, passport and courier instructions for Sri Lanka; verify the current exact checklist.
10. After arrival: commune declaration, medical check-up and student residence permit application before Visa D expires.

## 9. High-risk verification flags
- 1 October 2026 process change: Visa D-first for new applicants; old AST applications only transitional.
- Sri Lankan route: New Delhi Embassy via Honorary Consulate Colombo; not Swiss Embassy for long-stay D.
- Funds: 80% REVIS; household-dependent amount. EUR 777.76 is only an arithmetic reference using June 2026 adult amount.
- Work: current Guichet says average 15h/week with holiday/research exceptions; older EU Portal says no work for Bachelor first two semesters. Resolve before advising first-year Bachelor.
- Post-study: 9 months only confirmed for Master's and doctoral graduates, not ordinary Bachelor's.
- Visa D 6-month validity; separate post-arrival residence-permit formalities.
