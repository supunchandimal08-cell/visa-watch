# VisaWatch — UK Student Route Source Review

**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Scope:** Seven UK sources currently listed in `data/sources.json`  
**Review status:** Source identity and purpose reviewed; legal rule entries still require named human approval before client advice.

## Executive findings

1. The seven links are relevant official UK government / Home Office sources for the Student route.
2. The Student sponsor register was last updated on **24 November 2026** when checked on 25 November 2026. The source note has been refreshed to reflect this. The register can change frequently; check it again close to CAS and application.
3. HC 584 was published on 3 September 2026. Its Appendix Student maintenance amendments APP ST8–ST10 take effect on **30 November 2026**. The specified monthly maintenance figures change from £1,529 to £1,570 for London and from £1,171 to £1,203 outside London. The statement says applications made before 30 November 2026 are decided under the rules in force on 29 November 2026 for these changes.
4. The GOV.UK financial-evidence guidance page still displays the pre-change amounts and says it was last updated 31 December 2025. Do not copy its displayed figures into advice for applications on or after 30 November without checking the amended Immigration Rules and updated guidance.
5. This review does not approve every individual legal interpretation. Work conditions, evidence details, course eligibility and applicant-specific exceptions still require case-by-case review.

## Important date-controlled financial requirement

As checked on 25 November 2026:

| Study location | Until 29 Nov 2026 | From 30 Nov 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| London | £1,529 per month | £1,570 per month |
| Outside London | £1,171 per month | £1,203 per month |

These are monthly living-cost figures, subject to the route's maximum-month calculation and any applicable exception. Course fees are separate and must be checked against the CAS. Do not calculate an applicant's total funds from these monthly figures alone without checking course duration, outstanding tuition, dependants and exemptions.

**Transitional point:** HC 584 states that applications made before 30 November 2026 are decided under the Immigration Rules in force on 29 November 2026 in relation to APP ST8–ST10. Check the actual application date and any later official amendments before giving an individual calculation.

## Items requiring manual legal review before client use

- Appendix Student: current course eligibility, sponsor status, CAS validity, academic progression and any ATAS requirement.
- Work conditions: confirm the exact ST 26 condition applicable to the student's sponsor type, course level and study mode, term / vacation period, and prohibited work. Do not promise that every Student visa holder may work 20 hours.
- Financial evidence: confirm the required holding period, evidence timing, account regulation/access, parent funds and consent, official sponsorship, student loan evidence and nationality-based exemptions from the current guidance and Rules.
- Sponsor register: confirm exact legal entity, sponsor category and rating; an institution's marketing page or offer letter is not proof of a current Student sponsor licence or CAS allocation.
- Course and CAS: verify the exact course, level, mode, location, start/end dates, fees and CAS details.
- HC 584: recheck for further amendments and verify the correct transitional rule for the date the application is made.

## Data quality / next actions

- The source inventory still lacks structured `authority`, `jurisdiction` and `category` fields for almost all entries. This review records suggested values in this document only; it does not silently add unverified metadata to all source records.
- UK jurisdiction should be recorded as **United Kingdom**; the competent source owner for these seven sources is **Home Office / UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI)**.
- Suggested categories are shown in the table above. A data-schema update should be made only after the team approves a consistent controlled vocabulary.
- Keep all seven UK sources on manual-review status for client-facing legal interpretation until a named reviewer signs off the individual rules.
- Recheck the sponsor register at the point of CAS/application and recheck financial figures after 30 November 2026.
