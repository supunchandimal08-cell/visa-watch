# Hungary — Student Immigration Source Review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Country:** Hungary  
**Record status:** Pending human review  
**Applicant scope:** Sri Lankan / non-EEA applicant seeking a full-time higher-education qualification.

## 1. Standard route
Admission to a qualifying Hungarian state-recognised higher-education institution → apply for residence permit for the purpose of studies through the national D-visa/residence-permit process → after approval, travel on the single-entry D visa to collect the residence permit. This is not an ordinary Schengen C visa. For Sri Lankan applicants, D-visa appointment is arranged through the Embassy of Hungary in New Delhi and application/biometrics are lodged at VFS Colombo on the allocated appointment.

## 2. Eligibility and admission
NDGAP guidance says the student route covers full-time training at a state-recognised higher education institution (and qualifying preparatory training organised by that institution). Applicant must prove the purpose of stay with admission/student-status evidence and demonstrate appropriate language skills. Where tuition is payable, evidence of payment may be required. University entry, English language tests, tuition and deposit rules vary by programme.

## 3. Funds and sponsorship
Applicant must prove sufficient resources for subsistence during the intended stay and return/onward travel, together with accommodation and health-insurance conditions. The current NDGAP factsheet does not state one universal monthly EUR/HUF amount. Do not publish a fixed monthly minimum without a current written authority instruction. The Sri Lanka-specific Embassy checklist requests six months of sponsor bank statements, sponsor income evidence, family relationship/support documents and a sponsor declaration. Affidavit alone is not a substitute for primary financial evidence.

## 4. Residence permit duration and renewal
NDGAP states study residence permit validity is at least one year, or adjusted to the course if the training is shorter than one year, with a maximum of three years per document. It may be extended for one to three years, subject to eligibility; it cannot outlast the passport. Extension is filed in Hungary through Enter Hungary while the current permit is valid. NDGAP states the applicant must have resided in Hungary at least 90 days in any 180-day period for an extension.

## 5. Work rights
A student residence permit holder may work up to 30 hours/week during the study period. Outside the study period, full-time employment is allowed for a maximum of 90 days in a year. This is a statutory ceiling, not a guarantee of work or income.

## 6. Post-study pathway
A graduate may apply for the residence permit for seeking a job or starting a business if they still hold a valid study permit, have successfully completed their studies, and the intended job/business is appropriate to the qualification level. Apply from within Hungary through Enter Hungary at least 15 days before the study permit expires. The job-search/business-start permit is maximum nine months and cannot be extended. It is not automatic and does not guarantee employment.

## 7. Dependants
NDGAP says a third-country national holding or applying for a study residence permit, student mobility permit or certificate generally cannot receive a family-reunification residence permit. A narrow exception is described for a child born in Hungary to a student permit holder. Do not promise spouse/dependent sponsorship on the ordinary student route.

## 8. Sri Lanka application route
- Embassy of Hungary in New Delhi has consular jurisdiction for Sri Lanka.
- D visa appointment request: visahungary.del@mfa.gov.hu or consulate.del@mfa.gov.hu; the Embassy's D-type page also lists appointment.del@mfa.gov.hu.
- Sri Lankan D visa application and biometrics are lodged at VFS Colombo on the date allocated by the Embassy. An online consular interview may be arranged.
- Germany's representation for Hungary applies to short-stay Schengen C visas only; it is not the national study D visa route.
- Current VFS page lists study visa fee LKR 39,560; service charge LKR 29,490 without biometrics or LKR 33,180 with biometrics. Fees are time-sensitive and must be checked on payment day.

## 9. Sri Lanka document checklist (reconfirm latest revision)
The Embassy PDF currently lists: completed residence-permit application and study appendix; valid passport and previous passports; photographs; flight booking; accommodation for the stay; insurance; six months of sponsor bank statements; sponsor income evidence; family member support declaration; university/college acceptance; Sri Lankan school certificates, family registration and birth certificate in English legalised by Sri Lanka MFA; police character certificate; English CV; signed motivation letter; and consultant certificate if applicable. The authority may request further evidence. Do not treat this list as immutable.
