# VisaWatch — Counsellor daily checklist

Use this checklist as a proposed staff aid. It does not replace company policy, official immigration guidance or supervisor review. Until the secure client-record system is approved and released, use synthetic data only in the prototype.

## Start of day

- [ ] Sign in using my own authorised staff account; never share credentials.
- [ ] Check assigned follow-ups and upcoming client appointments.
- [ ] Review VisaWatch monitoring alerts and identify items marked “Needs review”.
- [ ] Do not treat a page-text change or publication-date change alone as a confirmed immigration rule change.
- [ ] Escalate unclear, conflicting or high-impact immigration information to the designated reviewer.

## Before each client consultation

- [ ] Confirm the client's identity using the company's approved process without copying unnecessary identity details into notes.
- [ ] Confirm the privacy notice and consent/lawful-basis record according to company policy.
- [ ] Understand the client's destination, study goal, education, English level, budget range and intended intake.
- [ ] Ask about relevant study gaps, previous refusals and other factors sensitively and only where relevant.
- [ ] Do not promise admission, visa approval, work permission, a job or earnings.

## During counselling

- [ ] Distinguish client-provided information from independently verified facts.
- [ ] Check the institution and exact campus are appropriately licensed/recognised for the proposed course.
- [ ] Check course entry requirements, duration, delivery mode, fees and refund terms from current official/provider sources.
- [ ] Check visa/residence requirements with the competent official authority for the relevant emirate/country and sponsor route.
- [ ] Check work permission separately; never assume a student residence permit automatically authorises work.
- [ ] Explain tuition, visa, insurance, medical, accommodation, travel and living costs separately where applicable.
- [ ] Record source URL, date checked, jurisdiction, relevant effective date and reviewer for important rule claims.

## After each consultation

- [ ] Update only the minimum necessary case information in the approved system.
- [ ] Update checklist status accurately: not requested, requested, received, verified or not applicable.
- [ ] Assign a clear next action, responsible person and follow-up date.
- [ ] Escalate uncertain legal/immigration interpretations for human review.
- [ ] Do not upload passports, NICs, bank statements, passwords or sensitive documents to the prototype or personal accounts.

## End of day

- [ ] Review overdue follow-ups and hand over urgent cases to the authorised colleague.
- [ ] Sign out of shared/workstation sessions and secure company devices.
- [ ] Report suspected data exposure, wrong-recipient messages or unauthorised access immediately.
- [ ] Do not export or transfer client information to personal email, messaging apps or devices.

## Supervisor review

- [ ] Review cases with uncertain eligibility, previous refusals, complex funding or unclear sponsor arrangements.
- [ ] Sample-check official-source citations and the date they were last verified.
- [ ] Confirm that no unreviewed proposed rule has been described to a client as confirmed.
- [ ] Review access and outstanding security issues according to company policy.

## Reminder

VisaWatch is an information and monitoring aid, not an automated legal decision-maker. A detected source change is not itself proof that an immigration rule has changed. Confirm the exact rule, jurisdiction, effective date and applicability before advising a client.
