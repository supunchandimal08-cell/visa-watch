# VisaWatch — Immigration rule verification checklist

Use this checklist before adding, changing, or explaining an immigration rule. It is a human-review control, not legal advice and not an automated approval.

## 1. Identify the exact rule

- [ ] Destination country and, where relevant, state, province, emirate or other jurisdiction are recorded.
- [ ] Applicant category is clear (for example: student, graduate, worker, visitor or dependant).
- [ ] Exact visa, permit, route, subclass or immigration category is identified.
- [ ] Applicant circumstances that affect the rule are noted (age band, course level, sponsor, institution type, application location), only where relevant.
- [ ] The claim is separated into individual facts rather than one broad statement.

## 2. Verify the source

- [ ] Prefer the responsible government department, immigration authority, legislation or official regulator.
- [ ] Confirm the page applies to the correct jurisdiction and applicant category.
- [ ] Record the exact official URL and page/document title.
- [ ] Record the date and time checked.
- [ ] Record the source owner / issuing authority.
- [ ] If an official page links to legislation, policy guidance or a formal instrument, inspect the underlying document where needed.
- [ ] Use university or provider pages only for provider-specific facts; do not use them as substitutes for immigration authority rules.
- [ ] If sources conflict or a source is unavailable, mark “Needs review” and escalate.

## 3. Record dates correctly

- [ ] Record publication date separately from last-updated date, if shown.
- [ ] Record the legal / policy effective date separately, only when confirmed by an authoritative source.
- [ ] Record transitional arrangements and who they apply to.
- [ ] Do not infer an effective date from a webpage update date.
- [ ] If no effective date can be confirmed, write “Not confirmed” and request reviewer assessment.

## 4. Classify the information

Choose one status:
- **Confirmed** — supported by a current authoritative source and reviewed.
- **Proposed / announced** — officially announced but not yet in force, or subject to implementation.
- **Needs review** — unclear, conflicting, incomplete, inaccessible or not yet checked.
- **Superseded** — replaced by a newer confirmed rule; retain the history and effective dates.

A detected webpage text change is only a monitoring signal. It must not automatically change a rule to “Confirmed”.

## 5. Verify common student-visa topics separately

- [ ] Institution, campus and course eligibility.
- [ ] Academic entry requirements and English-language requirements.
- [ ] Financial evidence, amount, currency, holding period and acceptable evidence.
- [ ] Tuition deposit and payment / refund conditions.
- [ ] Health insurance, medical examination and biometrics.
- [ ] Student work permission, hours and term-time / vacation conditions.
- [ ] Whether a separate work permit or employer application is required.
- [ ] Dependants, if relevant.
- [ ] Visa duration, renewal and post-study / grace periods.
- [ ] Application location, processing steps and required documents.
- [ ] Refusal, appeal or review options, only where officially confirmed.

Do not assume a student residence permit automatically authorises employment. Verify the exact permit and competent authority.

## 6. Human review and client communication

- [ ] A named reviewer has checked the source and interpretation.
- [ ] Reviewer name / staff ID and review date are recorded in the approved system.
- [ ] Any uncertainty or limitation is stated clearly.
- [ ] Client-facing wording is plain, accurate and does not promise approval, employment or income.
- [ ] The counsellor explains which details depend on the applicant's own circumstances.
- [ ] High-impact, ambiguous or conflicting issues are escalated before client advice is given.

## 7. Change monitoring and audit

- [ ] Compare the changed official text with the previous captured version.
- [ ] Identify the exact changed passage and whether it affects a rule, explanation, page layout or unrelated content.
- [ ] Check the official publication / instrument and effective date.
- [ ] Preserve the previous record and review history.
- [ ] Record who changed the rule, what fields changed and why.
- [ ] Do not delete or overwrite historical records without an approved retention process.

## 8. Release gate

A rule may be shown to counsellors as confirmed only after all required checks and human review are complete. If evidence is incomplete, keep it as “Needs review” or “Proposed / announced”.

VisaWatch detects and organises information. It does not make legal determinations or replace official immigration advice.
