# Italy Student Visa and Residence Permit — Official Source Review (25 November 2026)

**Status:** Country review refreshed; all VisaWatch Italy rules remain **Pending human review**. This is a counsellor reference, not a visa guarantee or legal decision.

## 1. Route for Sri Lankan applicants
- Non-EU applicants residing abroad generally apply for the appropriate Italian study visa at the Italian Embassy/Consulate responsible for their country of residence. Sri Lankan residents apply through the Embassy of Italy in Colombo's designated VFS process.
- The Embassy's official page says both national and Schengen visa applications require a prior appointment booked directly by the applicant through the official VFS appointment portal. The application is lodged at VFS Colombo, 675 Dr Danister De Silva Mawatha, Colombo 05. Appointment slots are opened periodically; the applicant must choose the correct category (Study) and cannot change it later.
- Do not use an intermediary to book or advise a client to select a false visa category to secure an appointment.
- A study visa is the entry authorisation. For a stay requiring a residence permit, the student must apply for the *permesso di soggiorno* within **8 days of arrival**.

## 2. Universitaly pre-enrolment and admission
- Non-EU citizens residing abroad who need a study visa to enrol in an Italian university must complete the applicable pre-enrolment procedure on **Universitaly**.
- The university evaluates and validates the application and provides the relevant eligibility information to the diplomatic mission. This validation is not a visa approval: the Embassy/Consulate has exclusive authority to decide the visa.
- Different study visa categories (university enrolment, single university course, postgraduate, exchange/mobility, traineeship/apprenticeship, vocational training, language study, research) may have different procedures. Confirm the exact course category and current academic-year instructions.
- For an Italian language course, MAECI says it must be higher-level, full-time and fixed-duration. Do not assume a short/general language course qualifies.

## 3. Financial means — academic years 2026–27 and 2027–28
- MAECI states that a person applying for a visa to enrol in an Italian university must demonstrate at least **EUR 10,179.85 per year** for academic years **2026–2027 and 2027–2028**. Where the degree lasts multiple years, the visa stage requires evidence for the first year; the applicant must still plan for the full course costs and future renewals.
- Return travel costs and all other costs must be considered separately. This is a minimum financial-means threshold, not a complete tuition/living budget.
- MAECI says bank guarantees or guarantee-insurance policies are not accepted as proof of financial resources for study stays. Personal/family guarantees or qualifying financial guarantees from Italian institutions/organisations and reliable foreign institutions may be considered.
- Colombo's checklist (version 3/2026, dated 3 September 2026) asks for traceability of funds and supporting evidence, including applicant/parent/relative sponsor income tax returns, employment/business evidence, salary slips, 12 months of bank statements and assets/property/vehicle documents. If a third person sponsors the applicant, the Embassy checklist asks for a relationship/explanation letter, employment/business proof, three months of sponsor bank statements and asset valuations.
- A third-party sponsor who is not family is not automatically sufficient. MAECI says an external person's hospitality and written maintenance commitment may be a positive but not decisive factor; the visa office decides.
- Recheck the amount and evidence for the exact visa category and academic year. Other study visas use a different calculation based on fixed and daily amounts.

## 4. Sri Lankan applicant document checklist
Embassy of Italy in Colombo's **Study Visa Checklist version 3/2026 (03 September 2026)** includes:
1. Completed and signed visa application form; ICAO-compliant photograph.
2. Current passport with at least two blank pages and validity at least three months beyond return to Sri Lanka; passport bio-data copy and applicant ID copy.
3. Applicant's own covering letter explaining the academic purpose and study plan in relation to prior qualifications.
4. For university Bachelor's, Master's, single-cycle and PhD applicants: Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) legalised by the Embassy or CIMEA certificate as an alternative; proof of Universitaly enrolment.
5. Evidence of the required financial means, source and traceability, sponsor documents where relevant, and scholarship evidence if applicable.
6. Institution's acceptance/enrolment letter with course duration/structure, accommodation information, grants and other relevant details; tuition payment receipt.
7. For Bachelor's, Master's and single-cycle degree courses: proof of language knowledge at **at least B2** in the language of instruction, even if the university has not expressly requested it.
8. Accommodation evidence.
9. Overseas medical insurance for at least 30 days, on the understanding that the student will enrol in an insurance scheme after arrival.
10. Bank letter concerning foreign exchange issuance after visa grant and recent credit-card statements; confirmed flight reservation; prior travel evidence if any.
- Sinhala, Tamil and other non-English documents must be accompanied by an official English translation and certified by Sri Lanka's Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The Embassy may request additional documents.
- Research-based study has a different supplementary checklist; confirm if the proposed research is paid work or a scholarship-only research visit.

## 5. Processing time and fees
- The Colombo student checklist says study visa processing may take up to **three months** and advises applying well in advance. The Embassy says the processing time cannot be expedited.
- The current VFS Italy Sri Lanka page lists **LKR 18,200** for a Type D long-term student visa and a VFS service charge of **LKR 6,370** per application. These local fees can change; verify the live fee table at booking/payment. Optional services may cost extra and are not compulsory.
- Do not confuse visa fee/VFS service charge with the separate Italian residence permit postal, administrative and card fees after arrival. Confirm the latest Questura/Poste Italiane fee schedule before payment; do not quote old general fee tables as current.

## 6. Residence permit after arrival and renewal
- Apply for the study *permesso di soggiorno* within **8 days** after arrival. The first application is commonly lodged using the residence-permit kit at a post office marked *Sportello Amico*; the Questura arranges fingerprinting and follow-up.
- Keep the postal receipt, appointment letter, copies of the application, passport, visa, insurance and proof of accommodation/funds.
- The permit is normally issued for the study period and must be renewed before expiry. The student must remain enrolled, maintain health insurance and adequate resources, and meet academic progress requirements.
- University-specific guidance commonly asks for at least one passed exam for the first renewal and two for subsequent renewals, subject to exceptions/force majeure; do not present this as a universal national threshold without checking the competent Questura and institution.
- Check local Questura instructions, renewal deadlines and current postal kit charges for the student's municipality.

## 7. Work during studies
- A valid study residence permit permits subordinate/employee work up to **20 hours per week**, with a maximum of **1,040 hours in any 52-week year**.
- Work rights do not guarantee a job or sufficient income. Self-employment, internships and research work may have separate conditions.
- Do not promise a student can start working immediately on arrival without complying with residence and employment formalities.

## 8. Post-study stay and conversion to work
Distinguish two separate routes:
- **Job-search permit:** Eligible non-EU graduates of qualifying Italian degrees (including Bachelor's, Master's, university Master's, PhD and specified higher qualifications) may apply for a *ricerca lavoro / attesa occupazione* residence permit for **9–12 months**, subject to the statutory requirements. The government integration portal describes registration with the local employment centre and the *Dichiarazione di immediata disponibilità al lavoro (DID)*, as well as means, accommodation and health coverage. Not every short course qualifies.
- **Direct work conversion:** A study permit that is still valid may be converted to subordinate or self-employed work where the applicant meets the relevant requirements and provides the required work proposal/contract or self-employment evidence. Since the 2023 Cutro reform, this conversion is no longer limited by annual *Decreto Flussi* quotas. This is not an automatic conversion; the permit must remain valid and the application is handled through the Ministry of Interior/Sportello Unico/Questura procedure.
- MAECI also says a graduate who already has a job offer can convert a still-valid study permit, with the work contract process at the Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione. Verify the precise current route with the competent Prefettura/Questura.

## 9. Counsellor checklist
1. Confirm applicant nationality, legal residence in Sri Lanka, exact course type, academic year and intended intake.
2. Confirm institution and Universitaly pre-enrolment requirement; do not treat university validation as visa approval.
3. Use the latest Embassy Colombo checklist and correct VFS category; applicant books appointment directly.
4. Calculate the 2026–27/2027–28 university funds amount only for the applicable category; verify source and traceability. Separate funds threshold from tuition, travel and living budget.
5. Check B2 language proof and Declaration of Value/CIMEA applicability.
6. Prepare official translations and Ministry of Foreign Affairs certification for Sinhala/Tamil documents.
7. Explain the 8-day post-arrival residence permit deadline, renewal and local Questura steps.
8. Explain the 20-hour/1,040-hour work limit; never guarantee work.
9. Explain job-search permit and direct work conversion as different conditional routes.
10. Keep all rules Pending human review until an authorised company reviewer approves.
