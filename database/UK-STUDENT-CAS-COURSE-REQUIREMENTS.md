# UK Student Route — CAS and Course Requirements

**Prepared:** 25 November 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom  
**Rule area:** Appendix Student ST 1, ST 7–ST 10, ST 13 and ST 23  
**Record status:** Needs human review before client advice  
**Primary authority:** Home Office / UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI)

## 1. Student route overview

The Student route is for a person aged 16 or over who wants to study with a licensed Student sponsor on an eligible course. A 16- or 17-year-old studying at an independent school on a qualifying course may have a choice between the Student and Child Student routes; assess the correct route rather than assuming.

An offer letter is not the same as a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS). A CAS reference is issued by the licensed Student sponsor and is required for a valid Student application, subject to the exact Rules.

## 2. CAS validity and sponsor checks

Before an application is prepared, verify:

- The sponsor is on the current Home Office Register of licensed sponsors: students.
- The exact legal sponsor entity and Student sponsor category match the student's course and campus arrangements.
- The sponsor licence remains valid when the application is decided.
- The CAS has not already been used in a previous application that was granted or refused.
- The sponsor has not withdrawn the offer after issuing the CAS.
- The CAS reference was issued no more than 6 months before the application date.
- The CAS contains the required course, qualification, level, place-of-study, fees and English-language information.

A sponsor register entry is not by itself proof that a particular course, campus, partner delivery arrangement or student is eligible. Confirm the actual CAS and sponsor confirmation.

## 3. Course requirements

Appendix Student ST 8–ST 10 sets the course, approved qualification and level-of-study requirements. Broadly, the course may need to be:

- A full-time degree-level or above course leading to an approved qualification; or
- A full-time below-degree-level course with at least 15 hours per week of classroom-based daytime study (08:00–18:00, Monday to Friday) leading to an approved qualification; or
- A qualifying full-time pre-sessional course; or
- Another specifically permitted course type, such as a qualifying Erasmus+ programme under the amended Rules.

The approved qualification and level rules have detailed conditions and exceptions. A marketing description such as “diploma”, “foundation”, “top-up” or “pathway” is not enough to decide eligibility. Check the actual awarding body, qualification level, delivery arrangement and the current Rules.

Probationary sponsors may be subject to additional course-level restrictions. Verify the sponsor's status and the applicable ST 10 requirements.

## 4. CAS course information

ST 23 requires relevant course information on the CAS, including as applicable:

- Course title and academic level.
- Course start and end dates.
- Weekly study hours and whether full-time or part-time.
- Main place-of-study address.
- Course fees and sponsor-provided accommodation costs.
- Reference details where the sponsor assessed the applicant using references.
- Work-placement details where the course includes a placement.
- Partner institution details where another provider delivers the course.
- Whether the ATAS requirement applies.

Check the CAS against the student's offer, deposit receipt, course confirmation and passport details. Resolve discrepancies with the sponsor before lodging.

## 5. English language

ST 13 generally requires English language ability at the required CEFR level in all four components:

- B2 for a course at UK bachelor's degree level or above.
- B1 for a pre-sessional course or a course below UK bachelor's degree level.

There are permitted ways to meet the requirement and sponsor assessment provisions. The CAS must record how the English requirement has been met and, where relevant, the assessment method or Secure English Language Test details. Check the applicant's exact course, nationality, prior study and any applicable exemption rather than assuming one test score fits all.

## 6. Other course-linked checks

- ATAS: verify whether the course and applicant require an ATAS certificate before the relevant study/application stage.
- Academic progression: if applying from within the UK, check the relevant progression provisions and exceptions.
- Work placement: confirm it is permitted under the Rules and properly recorded on the CAS.
- Partner delivery: verify the permitted partner institution and where study takes place.
- Course changes, deferrals and sponsor changes: obtain sponsor and immigration review before advising that the existing CAS or permission remains suitable.

## 8. Client-safe explanation

“Your university offer is an important step, but it is not the visa sponsorship document. Before applying, we need to confirm that the institution and exact course meet the UK Student route rules and that the sponsor has issued a valid CAS with the correct details. Admission and visa approval are separate decisions.”
