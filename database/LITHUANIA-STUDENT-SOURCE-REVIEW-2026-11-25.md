# Lithuania — Student immigration source review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Status:** Pending human review — not approved for client legal advice  
**Audience:** Sri Lankan third-country national applying for recognised higher education in Lithuania.

## Executive summary
Lithuania remains a possible study destination, but the route has material 2026 changes. Do not give old generic advice without checking the student's course level, year of study, residence-permit issue date and application location. In particular:
- Sri Lankan applicants cannot submit a Lithuanian TRP/national D visa application at the former VFS Sri Lanka centre; official sources say the centre closed for these long-stay applications from 1 March 2024. Singapore is listed by the Migration Department as a currently operating overseas TRP/national visa service location, but the applicant must verify eligibility for that centre and book through MIGRIS.
- From 22 May 2026, first- and second-year Bachelor students with TRPs issued after that date are restricted to 20 hours/week; third/fourth-year Bachelor, Master's and doctoral students may work up to 40 hours/week under the Migration Department notice. Study-based TRP total duration is capped at 8 years.
- From 1 November 2026, the rules for filing first-cycle Bachelor TRP applications from inside Lithuania tightened. A person who entered on a short-stay Schengen visa or another country's national visa cannot lodge that Bachelor TRP application in Lithuania; they must use an eligible VFS Global centre.
- Old EU Immigration Portal guidance (last updated 1 April 2025) still gives a blanket 20-hour work summary. It must not override the newer national 2026 notice.

## 1. Admission and study eligibility
- Applicant must be enrolled in a Lithuanian-recognised higher education institution or an eligible student exchange programme.
- The institution submits a mediation letter in MIGRIS; the applicant needs its mediation-letter number for the residence application.
- Confirm programme accreditation/recognition, full-time status, admission conditions, language requirements, tuition deposit and course duration directly with the university.
- Exchange students and degree students may follow different visa/residence routes. Confirm exact route for the course and intake.

## 2. Visa / residence route
- Degree students generally apply for a study-based temporary residence permit (TRP) through MIGRIS and an eligible external service provider; exchange students may use a national long-stay visa route.
- Do not assume a national D visa is universally required for a full-time degree student; current Migration Department process and the university's intake-specific instructions take priority.
- The EU Immigration Portal says a national long-term visa can be issued for up to one year and cannot be renewed; it describes a study TRP generally up to two years or the study duration if shorter. Check current law and MIGRIS for current validity and renewals.
- Renewal: older EU Portal guidance says lodge at least two months before expiry, not earlier than four months; confirm the current window in MIGRIS.

## 3. Sri Lanka application location — critical
- Lithuanian Migration Department officially announced that VFS Global stopped accepting Lithuanian TRP and national-visa applications in Sri Lanka from 1 March 2024.
- The current official overseas services directory lists Singapore as a TRP and national-visa application service location. It provides the VFS Singapore address and says appointments are booked through MIGRIS after status becomes “Submitted to external service provider”.
- Therefore, do not tell a Sri Lankan student to submit a study TRP at VFS Colombo. Confirm with Migration Department in writing that the applicant may use Singapore, check appointment availability, travel/visa requirements for Singapore, and required originals before paying.
- The VFS Sri Lanka webpage may still display study visa information and fees; that does not override the Migration Department's closure notice for TRP/national visa services. A short-stay Schengen C visa is a separate process and is not a long-term study residence permit.

## 4. Funds, insurance and documents
- Applicants must show sufficient means of subsistence and return travel, accommodation, and health insurance.
- Vilnius University currently publishes a planning figure for study TRP of at least EUR 577 per month for 12 months plus EUR 1,153 for return travel. For graduate job-search TRP it lists EUR 1,245/month. These are university guidance figures; check the live Migration Department amount and current ministerial order before quoting.
- EU Immigration Portal lists insurance cover of EUR 6,000; Vilnius University currently lists minimum insurance coverage of EUR 10,000. Because the official pages differ, use the current MIGRIS checklist and insurer policy accepted by Migration Department, not the lower figure by assumption.
- Core documents include passport, university mediation-letter number, bank evidence/income, accommodation evidence, health insurance, criminal-record certificate for first TRP where required, travel history and translations/legalisation as required.
- Sponsorship: do not assume a parent sponsor statement alone is sufficient; confirm the accepted format, account ownership and source of funds with Migration Department and the university.

## 5. Work rights — 2026 rules
Migration Department notice states that changes effective 22 May 2026 limit first- and second-year Bachelor students to 20 hours per week. Holidays and curricular internship are excluded from this cap. The 20-hour restriction applies to first/second-year Bachelor students whose Lithuanian TRP was issued after 22 May 2026.
- Third- and fourth-year Bachelor students: up to 40 hours/week under the notice.
- Master's students: up to 40 hours/week under the notice.
- Doctoral students and medical residents: up to 40 hours/week under the notice.
- Combined period for study-based TRPs may not exceed 8 years.
The 2025 EU Immigration Portal says 20h/week generally and has become outdated for these categories. Confirm current statute, permit issue date and course-year classification for each case. No job placement is guaranteed.

## 6. Post-study
- Eligible graduates may apply for a temporary residence permit for up to 12 months to seek employment or pursue self-employment.
- During the graduate job-search permit, eligible graduates may work without the student work-hour restriction, subject to the current law and permit conditions.
- Vilnius University publishes EUR 1,245/month as a graduate job-search maintenance planning figure; verify current MIGRIS requirements and state fee.
- This is a residence option, not a job guarantee.

## 7. Family / dependants
The EU Immigration Portal says student family members generally cannot accompany the student except doctoral students. This is an older general summary; verify current national family-reunification law and the doctoral student's eligibility. Do not promise spouse/child permits to ordinary Bachelor/Master students.

## 8. 1 November 2026 in-country filing change
From 1 November 2026, a person legally present in Lithuania can submit a study-based TRP application for first-cycle Bachelor studies directly to the Migration Department only if they hold:
- a valid Lithuanian TRP;
- a Lithuanian national D visa;
- a residence permit issued by another Schengen/EU Member State; or
- lawful visa-free status in Lithuania.
A person who entered on a Lithuanian/other Schengen short-stay visa or another country's national visa cannot lodge the first-cycle Bachelor TRP application in Lithuania and must apply through an eligible VFS Global centre. Confirm status and location before travel. This does not remove the need for admission, mediation letter or MIGRIS process.

## 9. Counsellor checklist
1. Verify institution recognition and programme type (Bachelor year 1/2/3/4, Master's, PhD, exchange).
2. Confirm university admission, intake, full-time status, tuition and mediation-letter number.
3. Check correct TRP vs national D visa route in MIGRIS.
4. Confirm applicant's application location; Sri Lanka VFS long-stay service is closed; verify Singapore eligibility.
5. Confirm current funds amount, account/sponsor evidence and return travel funds.
6. Verify health insurance minimum and coverage accepted by Migration Department.
7. Collect accommodation, police clearance, passport/travel history, translations and legalisation.
8. Apply through MIGRIS and book the assigned VFS appointment; do not book based on old third-party instructions.
9. Determine work limit using permit issue date and course year; explain no guaranteed employment.
10. Explain 12-month post-study route as an application option, not a job promise.
