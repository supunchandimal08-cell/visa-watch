# Greece — Student Immigration Source Review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Country:** Greece  
**Record status:** Pending human review  
**Scope:** Non-EU / third-country national applying from Sri Lanka for higher-education study.

## Executive route
For a qualifying full higher-education programme: obtain institutional admission → apply for a Greek national long-stay study visa (Type D) through the competent Greek consular authority → enter Greece → apply for the H.1 study residence permit. Do not use a short-stay Schengen C visa as the ordinary full-degree route. Confirm programme and institution eligibility with the university and Greek migration authority.

## Verified legal findings
1. **Admission and eligibility:** acceptance by a Greek higher-education institution for a specific programme; sufficient financial resources for maintenance and return travel; pay enrolment fees where required. Admission, language, recognition and tuition conditions are university-specific. [EU Immigration Portal](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/student-greece_en); [Migration Code](https://migration.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/%CE%9A%CF%8E%CE%B4%CE%B9%CE%BA%CE%B1%CF%82-%CE%9C%CE%B5%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%BD%CE%AC%CF%83%CF%84%CE%B5%CF%85%CF%83%CE%B7%CF%82.pdf).
2. **Funds:** the Code requires sufficient resources for living costs during the stay and return travel, assessed individually; grants/scholarships may count. The EU portal also requires evidence of income. A university information page mentions EUR 650/month, but this was not verified as a current nationwide statutory 2026 threshold. Do not publish EUR 650 as a guaranteed legal minimum. Obtain the current written checklist for the applicant's specific consular application.
3. **Permit duration:** Law 5275/2026, Article 30, amended Migration Code Article 107. H.1 permit duration aligns with the maximum prescribed duration of the programme and may be renewed if enrolment/continuing-study and statutory conditions remain satisfied. Full-programme permit holders must submit enrolment/participation and academic-progress evidence every two years. Renewal must be submitted before expiry.
4. **Residence permit fee:** EUR 150 under amended Article 107. This is not the Type D visa fee or visa-centre service fee.
5. **Work:** Law 5275/2026, Article 31, amended Article 118. H.1 holders may work part-time outside study hours, maximum 20 hours/week. No guarantee of employment or earnings.
6. **Post-study:** Law 5275/2026, Article 32, amended Article 119. Eligible graduates/researchers may apply for H.11, a one-year residence permit for job-seeking or starting a business, subject to continuing statutory conditions. It is not automatic and is not itself an unrestricted work permit.
7. **Sri Lanka route:** Greek MFA identifies Embassy of Greece in New Delhi as competent authority for Sri Lanka and lists a Greece Visa Application Centre in Sri Lanka. Colombo has an Honorary Consulate, but it is not the visa decision authority. Obtain written confirmation that the Colombo centre accepts the national Type D student visa category, appointment and document process.
8. **Documents/insurance:** use the current Type D checklist from the Embassy. Likely evidence includes passport, visa form, university acceptance/enrolment and fee evidence, funds, accommodation, insurance, and any criminal-record/medical certificates required. Legalisation and certified translation depend on document and consular instruction. Do not treat university guidance as the universal consular checklist.

## Important 2026 legal update
Law 5275/2026 was published in Government Gazette 17 on **6 February 2026** and its Article 69 provides commencement on publication unless a specific exception applies. Articles 30–32 changed the study permit duration, work ceiling and post-study status. The EU Immigration Portal profile is dated 1 April 2025 and includes older statements that graduates must return home and apply afresh; those statements must not override the subsequent enacted 2026 amendments.

## Open verification items for human reviewer
- Confirm current Type D visa fee and the national-visa checklist applicable to Sri Lankan residents.
- Confirm whether the Colombo GVC centre accepts D student visa applications, appointment route, original documents and service fee.
- Obtain current written funds evidence threshold/period and accepted sponsor/bank formats; do not reuse the unverified EUR 650 figure.
- Confirm insurance minimum cover and acceptable Sri Lankan policy format.
- Confirm institution/programme eligibility and exact post-study H.11 conditions with the Greek authority.
- Check transitional provisions for applications lodged before 6 February 2026.
