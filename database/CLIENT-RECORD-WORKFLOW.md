# VisaWatch — Proposed client-record workflow

This is a proposed staff procedure for a future approved client-record system. It is not evidence that secure storage or shared login is currently available.

## 1. Before creating a record

- Confirm the person has received the company's privacy notice and the required consent or other documented lawful basis has been recorded.
- Check whether the person is already in the system to avoid duplicate records.
- Collect only information needed for the counselling task.
- Use a generated client reference; do not use a passport number, NIC number or phone number as the record ID.
- Do not upload passports, identity cards, bank statements, passwords or other sensitive documents into the initial system.

## 2. Create and assign

- An authorised staff member signs in using their own account.
- Create a minimal record with age range, nationality, current residence, intended destination, broad education summary, English level, study interest, funding category and counselling stage.
- Assign one responsible counsellor and set a realistic follow-up date.
- Record only necessary, factual counselling notes. Avoid speculative or discriminatory comments.

## 3. Counselling and verification

- Confirm the client's goals, education, English level, budget and preferred intake.
- Separate verified facts from client statements and items still requiring evidence.
- Verify institution, campus, course, fees, entry requirements and immigration rules against official sources.
- Record the source URL, date checked, applicable jurisdiction, publication/effective date where available, and reviewer.
- Never promise admission, visa approval, work rights, employment or income.

## 4. Checklist and progress

Use checklist status only (for example: not requested, requested, received, verified, not applicable). In the first release, do not store the document itself or detailed document contents.

Update the application stage only when supported by evidence. Add a follow-up date when action is required.

## 5. Corrections, reassignment and closure

- Correct inaccurate information promptly and retain the required audit metadata.
- Reassign cases only through an authorised process.
- When the case closes, record the closure reason using an approved minimal category.
- Apply the company's approved retention and deletion schedule. Do not keep records indefinitely by default.

## 6. Access, incidents and exports

- Never share accounts or passwords.
- Report misdirected records, unexpected access, lost devices or suspected disclosure immediately through the company's incident process.
- Do not export, email or copy client data to personal devices or accounts.
- Admin access and staff membership must be reviewed regularly.

## 7. Release gate

This workflow may be adopted only after management and privacy approval, provider/hosting review, implementation of authentication and database row-level security, successful role-based security tests, backup/restore testing, staff training and written release sign-off. Until then, use only synthetic test data in the prototype.
