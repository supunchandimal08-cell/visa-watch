# Bulgaria — student residence source review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** Republic of Bulgaria (Schengen Member State)  
**Applicant lens:** non-EU/EEA applicant resident in Sri Lanka  
**Record status:** Pending human review — not legal advice

## 1. Correct route and scope
For a non-EU student undertaking full-time higher education in Bulgaria, the standard route is national long-stay visa Type D followed by a prolonged residence permit from the Ministry of Interior Migration Directorate after arrival. The EU Immigration Portal identifies full-time higher education as the student route. A short-stay Schengen C visa is not the ordinary degree-student permission. Secondary-school exchange students are a separate category. Confirm the exact university, recognised programme and full-time status.

## 2. Residence eligibility, validity and documents
Foreigners Act Article 24c provides that a foreigner holding a Type D visa and accepted for full-time study at a Bulgarian higher-education institution may obtain a prolonged residence permit for up to one year. A special rule allows 18 months when the permit relates to the final year of study. The student must submit the required evidence, including passport, accommodation, health insurance where applicable, sufficient means, initial criminal-record certificate, Ministry of Education admission certificate and university confirmation for the relevant academic year. Proof of paid enrolment fee is presented after entering Bulgaria with the Type D visa. Verify the current consolidated act and Migration Directorate checklist before filing.

## 3. Financial requirements — 2026 benchmark
Article 24c requires stable, regular, predictable and sufficient means for the period of residence, at least the national minimum monthly salary, minimum scholarship or minimum pension. Council of Ministers Decree No. 243/2025 sets the 2026 minimum monthly wage at **EUR 620.20** from 1 January 2026. This is a statutory monthly benchmark, not a full living-cost budget and not tuition. Calculate against the requested residence period and obtain written confirmation of acceptable evidence (own funds, sponsor, scholarship) from the competent authority. Do not count future part-time wages as proof of funds.

## 4. Student work rights
Under Article 38 of the Labour Migration and Labour Mobility Act, full-time third-country students at Bulgarian higher-education institutions may work up to **20 hours per week during the school year** and during officially declared university vacations. The employer must register the employment with the Employment Agency within seven days after it begins. This does not guarantee a job or income.

## 5. Post-study pathway
Article 24c(13) allows an eligible foreign national who completed full-time higher-education studies in Bulgaria and held the relevant prolonged residence permit to apply for a post-study prolonged residence permit of up to **nine months**. Apply at least 30 days before current authorised stay expires. The Migration Directorate checks that the graduate applied to the Employment Agency for job-seeker registration within seven working days after completing studies. Confirm the exact graduate's eligibility and evidence; it is not a job guarantee.

## 6. Visa fee and Sri Lanka filing route
The Bulgarian MFA lists **EUR 100** for a Type D visa under Article 15(1), subject to exemptions and changes. The MFA's Sri Lanka page states Bulgaria has no functioning embassy in Sri Lanka and the Embassy in New Delhi is accredited to Sri Lanka; consular appointments are made by email. Do not assume the honorary consulate in Colombo accepts or decides long-stay student visas. Obtain written confirmation from the New Delhi Consular Section on appointments, personal appearance, documents, legalisation, Bulgarian translation, fee payment and passport return before accepting a client application.

## 7. Tuition and living-cost planning
The European Education Area profile gives indicative annual tuition ranges of EUR 562–5,360 for Bachelor's and EUR 1,945–5,360 for Master's programmes, and typical living costs of EUR 500–900/month. These are estimates only; they are not national fixed tuition rates or the immigration minimum. Use the exact university fee schedule, offer letter, accommodation quotation and refund policy for client budgeting.

## 8. Counsellor verification checklist
- Confirm the exact institution and full-time higher-education programme and Ministry admission certificate.
- Check the latest consolidated Foreigners Act and Labour Migration Act before each application.
- Calculate subsistence evidence for the requested stay using the 2026 EUR 620.20 monthly floor; confirm the accepted evidence format.
- Confirm health insurance, accommodation, criminal-record certificate, translations and legalisation.
- Contact Embassy New Delhi for current Sri Lankan Type D appointment and filing arrangements; do not assume VFS Colombo.
- Explain the 20-hour work rule and employer's seven-day registration duty without promising employment.
- Assess nine-month post-study eligibility individually.
- Keep every legal interpretation in VisaWatch marked **Pending human review** until an authorised human reviewer approves it.
