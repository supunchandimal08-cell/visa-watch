# Slovenia — Student Immigration Source Review
**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Status:** Research draft — all dashboard rules remain **Pending human review**.  
**Scope:** Third-country (non-EU/EEA) applicants, especially Sri Lankan applicants.

## Route and eligibility
A temporary residence permit for study is generally required for stays over 90 days. The official InfoTujci portal describes eligible publicly recognised education programmes leading to recognised qualifications, recognised exchange programmes and authorised training. The permit is normally issued for the programme duration, up to one year, and may be extended if conditions remain met. Apply at an Administrative Unit if lawfully in Slovenia; otherwise the general route is through a Slovenian diplomatic/consular mission abroad. After collection, register temporary residence. [InfoTujci](https://infotujci.si/en/vsebina/drzavljani-izven-eu/temporary-residence-permit/based-on-studies/dovoljenje-za-zacasno-prebivanje-zaradi-studija/)

## Documents
Core evidence includes valid passport, enrolment/admission, health insurance, sufficient means and any case-specific evidence requested by the authority. Study in Slovenia additionally lists a recent home-country criminal-record certificate translated into Slovene and apostilled/certified where applicable. Confirm the current receiving-authority checklist, translation and legalisation requirements before lodgement. [Study in Slovenia checklist](https://studyinslovenia.si/live/visa-and-residence-permit/temporary-residence-permit-for-studies/)

## Financial means — unresolved discrepancy
Study in Slovenia pages still show EUR484.88/month and EUR494.09/month. GOV.SI's 2026 basic minimum-income table shows EUR507.43 from 1 March 2026. On 22 October 2026 the Government announced adoption of a **proposal** to increase general sufficient means to twice the basic amount (EUR1,014.86); this announcement alone does not prove enactment. Do not state a definitive student threshold until the current law and competent Administrative Unit's calculation are confirmed. [GOV.SI minimum income](https://www.gov.si/teme/denarna-socialna-pomoc/) · [Government proposal, 22 Oct 2026](https://www.gov.si/en/news/2026-10-22-17th-regular-session-of-the-government-of-the-republic-of-slovenia/)

## Work while studying
Student work is organised through authorised Student Services and a referral (napotnica). The study portal describes foreign-student registration requirements including Slovenian bank account, tax number and enrolment evidence. Ordinary employment is a separate route: the Employment Service describes employer vacancy notification, labour-market check and an information sheet, after which the Administrative Unit may issue a card indicating the right to work. No universal weekly hours cap is confirmed by the cited pages; do not promise jobs or unrestricted work. [Study in Slovenia — Working](https://studyinslovenia.si/live/working/) · [Employment Service — foreign residence-permit holders](https://www.ess.gov.si/en/jobseekers/employment-of-non-eu-migrant-workers/work-in-slovenia/foreign-nationals-holding-a-residence-permit)

## Post-study
Eligible third-country graduates who completed tertiary studies in Slovenia within the previous two years and obtained at least a tertiary qualification may qualify for a non-renewable job-seeking/self-employment residence permit of up to nine months. It permits job-seeking only; it is not itself work authorisation. A new appropriate permit is required before taking up employment/self-employment. [InfoTujci — post-study permit](https://infotujci.si/en/vsebina/drzavljani-izven-eu/temporary-residence-permit/based-on-studies/dovoljenje-za-zacasno-prebivanje-studentov-zaradi-iskanja-zaposlitve-ali-s samozaposlitve/)

## Sri Lankan consular route
The Slovenian Embassy in New Delhi is accredited to Sri Lanka. GOV.SI lists the Swiss Embassy in Colombo as Slovenia's **short-stay Schengen** representation; this is not evidence that the Swiss Embassy accepts Slovenian national D study visa or first residence applications. Obtain written confirmation from the Slovenian Embassy in New Delhi before advising where a student should lodge a long-stay application. [New Delhi Embassy](https://www.gov.si/en/representations/embassy-new-delhi/about-the-embassy-new-delhi/) · [Visa information](https://www.gov.si/en/representations/embassy-new-delhi/visa-information/)

## Counsellor approval checklist
1. Confirm exact provider/programme recognition and the student's admission.
2. Obtain current embassy instructions in writing for Sri Lankan D-study / first residence applications.
3. Reconcile student financial means against current law and any enacted amendment.
4. Confirm insurance, police certificate, translation and legalisation requirements.
5. Determine whether work is student-referral work or ordinary employment requiring the Employment Service process.
6. Explain the nine-month post-study route as job-search only, not a job or work-permit guarantee.
