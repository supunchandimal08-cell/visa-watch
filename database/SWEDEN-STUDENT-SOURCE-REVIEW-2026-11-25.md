# Sweden Student Residence Permit — Official Source Review (25 November 2026)

## Scope and status
For Sri Lankan non-EU/EEA nationals applying for first- or second-cycle higher-education studies (Bachelor's/Master's) in Sweden. A stay longer than three months generally requires a residence permit for studies. The permit must be issued before travel. For stays under three months, a residence permit is not the route; an entry visa may be required depending on nationality. Doctoral studies and other education types can have separate rules. All dashboard rules remain Pending human review.

## 1. Admission and tuition
- Applicant needs a valid passport and final admission to full-time, on-site studies. University/college full-time means 30 credits per semester. Distance-learning-only studies do not qualify under this route.
- Applicants who are required to pay tuition must pay the tuition fee before applying for the residence permit. The university notifies the Swedish Migration Agency after payment; only then is the admission considered final for this permit purpose.
- Non-EU/EEA/Swiss applicants normally pay a University Admissions application fee of SEK 900 per semester. This is separate from tuition and the residence-permit fee. Tuition varies by university and programme; quote only the actual institution's invoice/fee page.
- Use Universityadmissions.se for central application and programme selection where applicable. Check programme-specific entry, English-language, document and deadline requirements directly.

## 2. 2026 maintenance funds
- Minimum maintenance amount for applications made in 2026: SEK 10,656 per month, for the entire requested permit period.
- If the applicant receives free accommodation through the university or an international-exchange organisation, the amount may be reduced by SEK 4,736/month. If free food is provided, it may be reduced by SEK 2,960/month. These reductions require qualifying evidence and should not be assumed for private rental/meal arrangements.
- Additional monthly amount for accompanying family: SEK 4,440 for spouse/cohabiting partner and SEK 2,664 for each child.
- Funds may be demonstrated through personal bank assets, eligible scholarship, student grants/loans, qualifying organisational sponsor or employment income with an employment certificate and payslips. A private individual such as a parent is not accepted as a formal sponsor. Money from relatives must be transferred to an account in the applicant's name and accessible to the applicant.
- Personal bank statement must be issued no more than four months before the requested permit start date and include account holder, bank name, issue date, balance and currency. Credit, shares/funds and locked assets inaccessible during the permit period are not accepted as personal bank assets.
- Employment income evidence must be an actual employment certificate and payslips; a job offer alone is insufficient. Do not rely on future part-time work to make up a funding shortfall.

## 3. Work rights — major change from 11 June 2026
- For Bachelor's/Master's-level students whose residence permit decision is granted on or after 11 June 2026, the general maximum is 15 hours per week during semesters.
- Work may exceed 15 hours if it is part of the education (for example, an internship), involves student representation, administration, research or artistic research at/connected to the admitting higher-education institution, or takes place during June, July or August (summer months: no limit under this rule).
- A student who has completed a higher-education programme equivalent to at least two semesters and holds the diploma/certificate may work without the 15-hour restriction during the remaining validity of that permit, provided they are not applying for a new first-/second-cycle study permit.
- Exceeding the applicable limit may lead to revocation or refusal of extension. Do not tell clients the former unrestricted work right still applies. The new rules apply based on the decision date from 11 June 2026, even if the application was lodged earlier.

## 4. Study progress and address notification — effective 11 June 2026
- For full-time higher-education students, acceptable progress is at least 37.5 credits during the first year and 45 credits per year from the second year onwards. Insufficient progress can affect revocation/extension.
- Student must notify the Swedish Migration Agency of their address within 30 days after receiving a permit and again after changing address, unless they are already covered by completed population registration.
- The stricter work, progress and address-notification requirements do not apply to a permit decision granted before 11 June 2026 until the student applies for an extension; on extension, the new requirements apply.

## 5. Health insurance
- If studies last less than one year and the student is not registered in the Swedish Population Register, comprehensive health insurance is required for the whole stay. It must cover emergency and other medical care, hospitalisation, emergency dental care and medical repatriation/home transportation.
- If studies last at least one year, the student should register in Sweden's population register; this normally provides access to Swedish healthcare and dental care.
- Family members staying for a maximum of one year also need comprehensive health insurance under the family rules effective 1 October 2026.

## 6. Sri Lanka application route
- Sweden's mission responsible for Sri Lanka is the Embassy of Sweden in New Delhi. Sri Lanka-specific Sweden Abroad guidance says residence-permit biometrics must be submitted at the Embassy of Sweden in New Delhi during migration-section opening hours.
- Apply through the Swedish Migration Agency's online e-service after final admission and tuition payment (if tuition is payable). In the application, choose the Embassy of Sweden in New Delhi for passport presentation/biometrics or interview as instructed. The Migration Agency may request an in-person visit.
- The Sweden Abroad Sri Lanka page lists the Swedish Consulate in Colombo and states that residence permit cards can be collected there on appointment, Tuesdays to Thursdays 10:00–12:00. Confirm current card arrival and appointment instructions before travel. The honorary consulate does not replace the New Delhi biometrics step.
- Documents must be in Swedish or English. Translations must be certified and submitted with a copy of the original-language document.
- Common documents: passport copies, final full-time on-site admission decision, tuition-payment confirmation where applicable, financial evidence, comprehensive insurance if required, and any family/relationship documents for accompanying family members.

## 7. Permit length, processing and fees
- Higher-education permits can be granted for up to one or two years depending on the institution, but never beyond course duration or passport validity. Some listed institutions qualify for two-year permits. Check the current institution list.
- Current Migration Agency statistics displayed 75% of recently decided higher-education cases decided within three months. This is historical case data, not a guarantee or service standard.
- First application fee: SEK 1,500 for adults and SEK 750 for children under 18. The fee is generally non-refundable if refused. Specific scholarship and other exemptions are listed by the Agency.
- Apply as soon as final admission and tuition payment are complete; do not book non-refundable travel based on an assumed processing time.

## 8. After graduation — job-search permit
- A graduate who has completed the entire higher-education programme at a Swedish university/university college, normally at least two semesters, with all courses passed may apply for a residence permit to look for work or explore starting a business.
- Apply before the current study permit expires. The post-study permit can be granted for up to one year for Bachelor's/Master's graduates. Doctoral graduates may qualify for a different duration (one year to 18 months).
- The 2026 maintenance amount is SEK 10,656/month for the requested period; the applicant also needs comprehensive insurance if the continuous Swedish permit period is under one year.
- This is not a job guarantee or automatic work permit. If an employer is found, the applicant must meet the applicable route and current rules for changing to a work permit. From 11 June 2026, changing permit route from inside Sweden generally requires completion of at least two semesters at a university/university college.

## 9. Family members — rules effective 1 October 2026
- Eligible family members (spouse/registered partner/cohabiting partner and children under 18) may apply at the same time as the student, subject to funds and evidence.
- Additional maintenance: SEK 4,440/month for spouse/cohabiting partner and SEK 2,664/month per child.
- If family members stay for a maximum of one year, comprehensive health insurance is required. New legal-guardian requirements apply to certain children who are not the student's own child. Check the individual family case and the 1 October 2026 rules.

## Counsellor checklist
1. Confirm course type, course length, institution, on-site attendance, full-time status (30 credits/semester for university), final admission and tuition payment.
2. Calculate SEK 10,656 × requested months; add SEK 4,440/month for partner and SEK 2,664/month for each child; check qualifying free food/housing reductions only with evidence.
3. Check funds ownership/access. Parent funds must be transferred to an account in the student's name; a parent cannot be described as a formal private sponsor. Review bank statement date and all required fields.
4. Explain 15-hour/week semester work cap for decisions from 11 June 2026, summer exception and other narrowly defined exceptions. Do not use the old unlimited-work explanation.
5. Check study progress (37.5 first-year credits; 45 credits per year thereafter) and 30-day address notification.
6. Confirm insurance if under one year; population registration if one year or more.
7. For Sri Lankan residents, arrange online application and New Delhi biometrics; separately confirm Colombo consulate card collection appointment.
8. Explain the one-year post-study job-search permit only after confirming complete higher-education programme and application before current permit expiry.
9. Keep all rules Pending human review until the authorised reviewer approves them.

## 10. Targeted official-source recheck — 26 November 2026

### Current primary source recheck
- Re-opened the Swedish Migration Agency's live higher-education residence-permit page and its current 2026 maintenance and work sections. The live page confirms the rules already captured in the 25 November review; the key figures and work limit remain current on 26 November 2026.
- For applications in 2026, maintenance is at least SEK 10,656 per month. A 12-month student-only calculation is SEK 127,872 (10,656 × 12); this is a calculated maintenance minimum, not a full tuition-plus-living budget. Qualifying institution/approved-organisation-provided free food can reduce the monthly figure by SEK 2,960 and qualifying free housing by SEK 4,736, with evidence.
- Bank assets must be in an account belonging to the applicant (and, where permitted, accompanying partner) and accessible during the requested permit period. The statement must be no more than four months old on the requested permit start date. Credit, shares/funds, and locked money unavailable during the permit period do not qualify.
- Private individuals, including parents, are not accepted as formal sponsors. A relative's contribution must be transferred to a bank account in the applicant's name and accessible to them. A job offer alone is insufficient: claimed employment income requires an employment certificate and payslips. Do not count speculative future part-time earnings or expected job income as available maintenance funds.
- For Bachelor's/Master's-level students whose residence permit was granted on or after 11 June 2026, paid work is generally limited to 15 hours/week during semesters. Work may exceed this limit for June, July and August, work integrated into the education, specified student representation/administration/research at the admitting institution, artistic research at that institution, and the limited post-completion remainder of an existing permit after a programme equivalent to at least two semesters has been completed. Exceeding the permitted hours can result in permit revocation or refusal of an extension. Do not apply this exact first-/second-cycle rule indiscriminately to doctoral or other education categories.
- The 11 June 2026 rules also require progress of at least 37.5 credits in the first year and 45 credits per subsequent year for applicable permits, and address notification within 30 days of receiving the permit and after a change of address, subject to population-registration rules. Transitional treatment for permits granted before 11 June 2026 must be checked at extension.
- The current family-member page, updated for rules effective 1 October 2026, confirms comprehensive health insurance for family members staying up to one year and the legal-guardian condition for certain children; it explicitly states there is no change to student-family maintenance amounts. The existing SEK 4,440/month partner and SEK 2,664/month per child amounts remain the stated 2026 additions.
- The Sri Lanka-specific Sweden Abroad page confirms biometrics for residence-permit applications must be submitted at the Embassy of Sweden in New Delhi. It lists Colombo consulate permit-card collection on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays by appointment, 10:00–12:00. Contact the consulate before travel and confirm the card has arrived; the Colombo consulate does not replace the New Delhi biometrics step.
- Higher-education study permits require final full-time on-site admission, 30 credits per semester for university full-time study, and tuition payment before applying where tuition is due. Under-one-year studies require comprehensive health insurance if the student is not population-registered; one-year-or-longer studies should register in Sweden's population register.
- Eligible post-study applicants must complete the full higher-education programme lasting at least two semesters with passing grades and apply before the current permit expires. The 2026 job-search maintenance figure is SEK 10,656/month. This route is not a job guarantee.
