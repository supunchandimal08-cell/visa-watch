# Cyprus Student Entry Permit and Residence — Official Source Review (26 November 2026)

## Scope and classification
Cyprus is an EU member state but is **not part of the Schengen Area**. A Cyprus student entry permit / temporary residence permit is a national Cyprus immigration route. It is not a Schengen student visa and does not itself provide Schengen travel rights.

This review is for third-country nationals seeking full-time higher education in Cyprus for more than 90 days, with specific notes for Sri Lankan applicants. All VisaWatch rules remain **Pending human review**.

## 1. Eligibility and entry permit
- The Cyprus Migration Department says a third-country national admitted to a full-time course leading to a higher-education qualification recognised by the Republic may enter with an entry permit issued by the Migration Department.
- The higher-education institution must be registered/recognised under Cyprus law.
- The educational institution submits the entry-permit application to the Migration Department.
- The application is submitted while the student is outside Cyprus or already holds a valid residence permit. It cannot be submitted while the student is in Cyprus as a tourist/visitor.
- After arrival, the student must register in the Aliens Register and apply for a temporary residence permit within **7 days**. Biometrics (photo/fingerprints/signature) are required.
- The student residence permit is generally valid for up to **one year** and renewable annually until the programme is completed, subject to continuing eligibility and satisfactory academic progress.

## 2. Financial evidence
- The general Migration Department student page states that financial evidence is determined by Ministerial Council decisions; it does not publish one universal fixed student balance for all institutions.
- University of Cyprus (UCY) provides a current institution-specific example for non-EU applicants:
  - Attested bank statement with at least **EUR 7,000** available in the month before application; or
  - Bank confirmation letter valid for six months showing the student's ability to cover EUR 7,000; or
  - Sponsor bank letter valid for six months. Sponsor is limited to father or mother and relationship must be proven.
  - UCY reduces the figure to **EUR 5,000** for a scholarship of 50% or more; full scholarship means no proof of financial ability under that UCY checklist.
- UCY after-arrival residence-card checklist separately asks for a local Cyprus bank confirmation showing at least **EUR 800** to cover living costs and tuition for the period requested.
- These are UCY-specific figures and must not be generalised to all Cyprus universities or colleges. Obtain the student's exact institution checklist and current financial decision before quoting a fixed amount.
- Tuition, accommodation, health insurance, medical checks, repatriation guarantee and travel must be separately budgeted where required.

## 3. Student work rights — 2026 Decree KDP 126/2026
- The Cyprus Government service page states third-country students may work only after completing their first academic semester and enrolling in the second semester; Foundation and high-school students are excluded.
- The student must be enrolled full-time in a recognised higher-education institution/programme and hold a valid student residence permit.
- The 6 March 2026 revised Decree KDP 126/2026 specifies occupations/sectors in which student employment is permitted. It is not unrestricted access to all jobs.
- Maximum hours: **20 hours per week** during teaching periods and **38 hours per week** during class breaks.
- Before starting, the student must have a specific employer contract, a monthly work schedule that does not clash with study hours, and Department of Labour approval through the prescribed online process. The contract is checked/stamped by the Department of Labour.
- Do not promise employment or count expected part-time income as the primary source of study funds.

## 4. Post-study stay
- The Migration Department states that after completing studies, an eligible graduate may apply for a residence permit of up to **12 months** to seek employment or establish a business.
- The work/business sought must correspond to the level of studies completed.
- The minimum qualification is **European Qualifications Framework (EQF) level 7** — Master's degree or higher. Ordinary Bachelor's graduates should not be told that they automatically qualify.
- Confirm the application category, financial evidence, insurance and timing with the Migration Department before the student permit expires. This is not a job guarantee.

## 5. Fees and processing targets
The Migration Department fee schedule lists:
- Entry permit + residence permit: **EUR 70**.
- Entry permit + residence permit + Alien Registration Certificate (ARC): **EUR 140**.
- Residence permit issuance/renewal: **EUR 55**.
- First registration in the Aliens Register: **EUR 70**.
- Complete student entry-permit applications: target examination within **one month**.
- Complete student residence-permit applications: target examination within **three months**.
- Incomplete files or requests for further information can take longer. UCY may collect the EUR 140 entry/residence/ARC amount for its own route; follow the institution's payment instructions and avoid double counting.

## 6. Sri Lankan application route
- The Cyprus High Commission in New Delhi has parallel accreditation for Sri Lanka.
- The High Commission's published page states a VFS Global service contract effective **21 July 2026** includes Sri Lanka among parallel-accreditation countries. The listed VFS centres are in India; the page does not list a Colombo VFS centre.
- The Migration Department says the higher-education institution submits the student entry-permit application. The student must get written instructions from the admitting institution and the High Commission about whether a passport visa sticker/endorsement or VFS appointment is required for the Sri Lankan applicant and where to submit it.
- Do not send original documents or pay optional VFS services before the competent office confirms the exact student entry-permit process.
- This is a Cyprus national entry/residence process, not a Schengen C-visa application.

## 7. Documents, medical screening and accommodation
- The Migration Department requires supporting documents to be officially translated and duly certified.
- UCY's current non-EU checklist is an institution-specific example and includes:
  - Criminal-record / good-conduct certificate.
  - Medical certificates including Hepatitis B/C, HIV, Syphilis and chest X-ray for tuberculosis.
  - Admission letter, academic certificates and English-language evidence.
  - Bank and sponsor evidence, where applicable.
  - Certified/legalised documents; UCY says apostille is required for applicants from Hague Apostille Convention countries.
- UCY's after-arrival checklist includes the entry permit, medical tests, health insurance covering inpatient/outpatient care, local bank evidence (minimum EUR 800 in its checklist), and certified one-year rental agreement/title deed.
- Other institutions may require different documents, balances, medical providers and accommodation evidence. Use the exact current institution checklist and Migration Department instructions.

## Counsellor safeguards
1. Label Cyprus as **EU / non-Schengen**. Do not market it as a Schengen student visa.
2. Verify the institution is recognised and the course is full-time and leads to a recognised higher-education qualification.
3. The institution submits the entry-permit application; do not advise a student to enter as a tourist and convert status.
4. Never quote EUR 7,000 as a universal Cyprus student funds threshold; it is a University of Cyprus checklist example.
5. Explain work eligibility only after first semester and second-semester enrolment, in listed occupations, with a specific employer contract and Labour Department approval.
6. Explain post-study stay is up to 12 months only for EQF level 7 or above; not a universal Bachelor's graduate route.
7. Confirm the Sri Lanka / New Delhi / VFS submission route with the university and High Commission before document dispatch.
8. Keep all legal rules **Pending human review** until approved by an authorised reviewer.
