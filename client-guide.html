Your journey, step by stepඔබේ ගමන පියවරෙන් පියවර

A practical starting point before you pay an application fee.Application fee ගෙවීමට පෙර අනුගමනය කළ යුතු මූලික පියවර.

01 — Plan your pathway

1
Know your goal

Choose your subject, qualification level and long-term study or career goal.

2
Check entry requirements

Compare your academic results, English level and any portfolio or experience requirements.

3
Build a full budget

Include tuition, visa, insurance, travel, accommodation and living costs—not just the first payment.

4
Verify before paying

Get written fee details, refund terms and the institution's official offer or invoice.

5
Apply and follow conditions

Submit truthful documents and follow the current instructions from the official authority.

02 — Documents to prepare

Passport and identity details
School / A-Level certificates
Academic transcripts
English test or evidence (if required)
CV or work history (if relevant)
Personal study statement (if required)
Sponsor and financial evidence
Institution offer and fee documents
Medical / insurance records (if required)
Translations or certified copies (if required)
Keep documents genuine. Never alter bank statements, certificates or employment records. Ask the institution or authority what format is accepted.

Understand the real cost

Ask for an itemised estimate covering tuition deposits and balance, visa and government charges, medical checks, insurance, flights, accommodation deposits, monthly living costs and an emergency reserve.

Part-time work is not a funding guarantee. Permission, hours and conditions vary by destination and visa. Do not plan to pay tuition or essential living costs using work you have not yet legally secured.

What to ask your counsellor

?
Is the institution officially recognised?

?
What is the complete first-year cost?

Request written tuition and living-cost assumptions.

?
What happens if the visa is refused?

Read the written refund and deferral policy.

?
What are the exact work conditions?

Confirm them with the official immigration or labour authority.

Dubai student route — Sri Lanka applicant checklistDubai Student Visa — ශ්‍රී ලංකාවේ අයදුම්කරුවෙකුගේ checklist

A counsellor workflow from first enquiry to post-arrival checks. Complete each checkpoint before moving on.පළමු විමසීමේ සිට Dubai පැමිණීමෙන් පසු පරීක්ෂා කිරීම් දක්වා counsellor අනුගමනය කළ යුතු ක්‍රියාවලිය.

01 — Before accepting an application

1
Screen the student

Record age range, nationality, current residence, A/L or highest qualification, stream, results, English level, study gap, previous refusals and intended intake.

2
Match a suitable course

Confirm entry requirements, course level, duration, campus, attendance mode and progression route in writing with the provider.

3
Verify the institution and campus

Check the legal provider, exact Dubai campus and relevant education authority approval (for example KHDA or the applicable authority). Do not rely on an agent brochure alone.

4
Confirm the visa sponsor route

Ask the institution who will act as host/guarantor, which authority handles the residence application (GDRFA or ICP as applicable), and what happens if the student changes or stops the course. Obtain written confirmation.

5
Issue a complete cost and refund sheet

Separate tuition, application/deposit, visa and government fees, medical, Emirates ID, insurance, accommodation, living costs, flights and emergency reserve. Use current written quotations; never invent a minimum bank balance or promise a refund.

02 — Application and arrival

6
Collect and quality-check documents

Use the provider's current checklist. Check passport validity, certificates, transcripts, translations, sponsor evidence and any required declarations. Never edit or fabricate evidence.

7
Review the offer before payment

Match student name, course, campus, intake, tuition, deposit, payment deadline, conditions and written refund/deferral policy.

8
Track the visa application

Record who submits it, the authority, application/reference number, submission date and any additional-document request. Submission is not approval.

9
Check permission before travel

Verify the issued entry permission, names, validity dates, permitted purpose and instructions directly with the sponsor/authority. Do not book non-refundable travel based only on an application receipt.

10
Complete post-arrival requirements

Follow the sponsor's instructions for medical fitness, Emirates ID, health insurance and residence permit issuance. Keep copies and due dates in the approved company system.

Work is a separate checkpoint. A student residence visa does not automatically authorise employment. Before any internship or job starts, verify student eligibility, employer jurisdiction (MoHRE or relevant Free Zone authority), the correct work/training permit, required institution or sponsor NOC, and the issued permit. Never promise a job, fixed weekly hours or earnings.
Grace-period caution: Do not quote a universal 60-day or 180-day period. GDRFA Dubai and ICP publish different provisions for different permit categories and circumstances. Confirm the student's exact emirate, sponsor, permit type and current status with the responsible authority before advising.

Provisional counsellor assessment

1
Academic pathway — verify

Three A/L passes may meet some provider entry routes, but eligibility depends on the exact course, grades, subject prerequisites and institution. Obtain written confirmation before recommending a level.

2
English readiness — development needed

Basic English may be below direct-entry requirements for some programmes. Ask the provider about its accepted test, internal assessment or approved English pathway, including extra cost and duration.

3
Financial readiness — not yet established

Collect a written itemised quote and a realistic monthly living budget. Confirm sponsor relationship, willingness and acceptable evidence directly with the provider/authority. Do not assume one universal UAE bank-balance threshold.

4
Work expectation — do not rely on it

Explain that a job is not guaranteed and work requires the correct permit and any required NOC. The student must be able to fund study and living costs without assumed part-time income.

Recommended next action: Put the case at “Eligibility review / more information required”. Request A/L certificate and detailed results, passport validity, English assessment, intended subject, preferred intake, sponsor funding plan and full available budget. Then shortlist only verified institutions/courses that confirm eligibility and visa sponsorship in writing.
Do not tell the client: “You will definitely get admission”, “visa is guaranteed”, “you can easily get a part-time job”, or “your job will pay your fees”. Instead explain what is known, what is still unverified, and who must confirm it.

Internal counsellor quality gate — before submission

1
Identity and consistency

Names, passport details, dates and translations checked against originals; no altered or misleading evidence.

2
Academic and course fit

Written provider confirmation of entry, English, course level, campus, intake and any conditions.

3
Institution and visa route

Legal provider/campus, sponsor or host, responsible authority and application owner documented.

4
Financial and client understanding

Itemised costs, funding source, refund policy and work-permit limitations explained; student understands that admission and visa are not guaranteed.

5
Second-person review and evidence

Reviewer records outstanding items, decision, date and official source links in the approved company system. Do not store passport scans, bank statements or sensitive client data in this public website.

Stop and escalate: Do not submit if identity documents conflict, provider or campus approval is unclear, sponsor/visa route is not confirmed, funds are unexplained, or the student is relying on a promised job to afford the course. Record what is missing and obtain written clarification.
