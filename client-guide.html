A practical starting point before you pay an application fee.Application fee ගෙවීමට පෙර අනුගමනය කළ යුතු මූලික පියවර.

01 — Plan your pathway

1
Know your goal

Choose your subject, qualification level and long-term study or career goal.

2
Check entry requirements

Compare your academic results, English level and any portfolio or experience requirements.

3
Build a full budget

Include tuition, visa, insurance, travel, accommodation and living costs—not just the first payment.

4
Verify before paying

Get written fee details, refund terms and the institution's official offer or invoice.

5
Apply and follow conditions

Submit truthful documents and follow the current instructions from the official authority.

02 — Documents to prepare

Passport and identity details
School / A-Level certificates
Academic transcripts
English test or evidence (if required)
CV or work history (if relevant)
Personal study statement (if required)
Sponsor and financial evidence
Institution offer and fee documents
Medical / insurance records (if required)
Translations or certified copies (if required)
Keep documents genuine. Never alter bank statements, certificates or employment records. Ask the institution or authority what format is accepted.

Understand the real cost

Ask for an itemised estimate covering tuition deposits and balance, visa and government charges, medical checks, insurance, flights, accommodation deposits, monthly living costs and an emergency reserve.

Part-time work is not a funding guarantee. Permission, hours and conditions vary by destination and visa. Do not plan to pay tuition or essential living costs using work you have not yet legally secured.

?

?
