Course & provider discovery

Course research

Start with official or government-supported search tools. Then verify the exact campus, course, entry requirements, tuition, duration and international-student eligibility with the institution.

🎓
Provider registration is not the same as course admission or visa approval.

Record the exact course and campus. Confirm fees and entry requirements on the provider’s own current page, and check the destination’s relevant register or official guidance before counselling.
